01

Marketing for a city that runs on visuals

Dubai is one of the most visually competitive markets anywhere. Hotels, restaurants, developers, beauty clinics and retail brands all compete for the same scroll, often with big production budgets. A marketing plan here has to assume your content will sit next to the best in the world, and your audience is international: residents from dozens of countries, tourists and investors who often find you on Instagram or Google Maps before anything else.

YKS Productions works as a compact marketing agency for Dubai brands. I set the strategy, plan the channels and shoot the content myself. My background includes campaign and content work for Marriott and Vaseline (Unilever), and a campaign reel that reached 3.3 million views. You deal directly with the person doing the work.

02

What Dubai clients usually need

Hospitality and F&B: Instagram and TikTok reels, Google Business Profile, Meta ads around launches and seasonal offers. Real estate: listing films, developer launch campaigns, lead forms on Meta and search campaigns on Google for project names. Beauty, wellness and clinics: trust-building content, UGC-style testimonials (with consent) and search ads for treatments. Founders and consultants: LinkedIn personal branding for a business audience across the GCC.

03

Bilingual and multicultural audiences

Many Dubai campaigns need to work for English- and Arabic-speaking audiences, as well as for large South Asian and European communities. I plan creative that relies on visuals and simple on-screen text first, so it travels across audiences, and work with your translators or Arabic copywriters where campaigns need native-language versions.

04

On the ground across Dubai

Shoots across Downtown, Business Bay, DIFC, Dubai Marina, JLT, Palm Jumeirah, Jumeirah, Al Quoz studios and newer communities in Dubai South and Dubailand. Strategy sessions in person or online.

05

Getting started in Dubai

Most Dubai engagements begin with a short audit of your Instagram, Google Business Profile, website and any ad accounts, followed by a written plan. The first shoot usually follows within a couple of weeks, so new content is ready when campaigns launch. Peak seasons (the cooler months, Ramadan and Eid, year-end) are planned in advance because production and ad competition both get busier.

Dubai questions

Are you a full marketing agency in Dubai?

A compact one. Strategy, ads and content production are handled by me directly, and specialists are brought in for things like Arabic copywriting or drones when a project needs them.

Which ad platforms work best in Dubai?

Instagram and Meta ads for hospitality, beauty and lifestyle; Google Search for high-intent services; LinkedIn for B2B. Most Dubai brands benefit from Meta plus Google.

Can you help with Google Maps visibility?

Yes. Google Business Profile setup and optimisation, fresh photos, posts and a review-request process are part of local SEO.

How do quotes work?

Monthly retainers or project fees based on scope. You get one written quote before work starts.
