01

Why it matters

In Dubai, LinkedIn is where hiring managers shortlist, where B2B buyers check who they are dealing with, and where founders build authority across the GCC. A profile with a vague headline, an empty About section and a cropped holiday photo quietly loses those opportunities.

Optimising it is part writing, part search and part visual. The writing has to say clearly what you do and for whom. The search side means using the words recruiters and clients actually type. The visual side is a headshot and banner that look professional at thumbnail size, which is where most people see you first.
