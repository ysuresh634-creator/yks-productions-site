Questions, answered straight

How do I join a modelling roster in Chennai?

Send your details and photographs through the application form. You do not need professional pictures to apply — clear, unedited photographs taken on a phone in daylight are enough to assess, and are what we ask for first. Applying is free and stays free. If you are put forward for a job, you are paid for that job.

Does YKS charge Chennai models a joining or portfolio fee?

No. There is no joining fee, no registration fee, no portfolio package and no compulsory shoot. Anyone in India asking a model to pay to be represented is running a different kind of business, and it is the clearest warning sign there is.

Can brands book a model in Chennai directly?

No, and that is deliberate. Talent contact details are never published and are never passed on. A brand sends a brief, receives a shortlist, and the booking is arranged through YKS Productions — which is what keeps talent safe and the shoot organised.

I am not from Chennai — can I still apply?

Yes. The roster is open to talent anywhere in India, not only Chennai or the cities listed. Where you are based matters for local jobs, but a brief that fits you can come from anywhere, and travel is arranged as part of the booking.

Do I need experience or an existing portfolio?

No. New faces are cast constantly, and every model on any roster started without a credit. What is actually required is being over 18, being reachable, and turning up when you say you will.

What does it cost to cast a model in Chennai?

It depends on the shoot — the number of days, the usage you need and how many people you are casting. Send the brief and you get one all-in number back covering the talent and the arrangement, so there is nothing to reconcile afterwards.
