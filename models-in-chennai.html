Does YKS Talents offer modelling jobs in Chennai?

No. YKS Talents does not offer jobs, placements or guaranteed work in Chennai or elsewhere. It is a portfolio platform where clients casting in Chennai find the right talent. Enquiries come through YKS Talents, so your contact details stay private.

Are there joining or registration fees for YKS Talents in Chennai?

YKS Talents is 100% free, with no fees ever. None. There is no joining fee, no registration fee, no portfolio fee and no paid test shoot. The portfolio builder is free and the PDF is yours to keep. Anyone asking for money in our name is not us.

Who can join YKS Talents in Chennai?

Models, influencers, content creators and actors aged 18 to 34 who live in or regularly work in Chennai, with a polished presence suited to silk, jewellery, beauty and contemporary fashion work. Every application is read personally.

What should a Chennai portfolio include for saree and jewellery work?

At least one well-draped saree look with jewellery clearly visible, a contemporary look for range, a close beauty frame and current natural-light digitals with accurate measurements. Clients here look closely at posture and hands, so choose frames where both are clean.

Does joining YKS Talents guarantee work in Chennai?

No. YKS Talents is a 100% free platform that makes your portfolio visible to brands and clients looking for talent in Chennai. When a client wants to book you, the booking is arranged through YKS, but no work is ever promised.

How do brands find talent in Chennai through YKS Talents?

Send the brief — the collection, the dates and how the images will be used. You receive a shortlist of suitable Chennai profiles and one all-in number. Talent contact details are never published or passed on.

Send a brief on the casting brief form with the look, the dates, the location in Chennai, the number of looks and where the images will run. There is no single rate. Fees are agreed per project and depend mostly on usage (how long, where and on which channels the images run), plus shoot length, number of looks, travel and any exclusivity. Browsing YKS Talents and sending a brief are free. More on what moves the cost.
