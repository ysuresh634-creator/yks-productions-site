No. YKS Talents does not offer jobs, placements or guaranteed work in Chennai or elsewhere. It is a portfolio platform where clients casting in Chennai find the right talent. Enquiries come through YKS Talents, so your contact details stay private.

Are there joining or registration fees for YKS Talents in Chennai?

None. There is no joining fee, no registration fee, no portfolio fee and no paid test shoot. The portfolio builder is free and the PDF is yours to keep. Anyone asking for money in our name is not us.

Who can join YKS Talents in Chennai?

Women aged 18 to 34 who live in or regularly work in Chennai, with a polished presence suited to silk, jewellery, beauty and contemporary fashion work. Every application is read personally.

What should a Chennai portfolio include for saree and jewellery work?

At least one well-draped saree look with jewellery clearly visible, a contemporary look for range, a close beauty frame and current natural-light digitals with accurate measurements. Clients here look closely at posture and hands, so choose frames where both are clean.

Yes. I cast my own YKS Productions shoots in Chennai from YKS Talents, including textile and jewellery campaigns. Being on the platform makes you visible for those castings and to clients; it is not a promise of selection.

How do brands find talent in Chennai through YKS Talents?
