04

Common questions

Do you travel to Hyderabad for single-day shoots?

Yes — I'm based between Bangalore and Dubai, and Hyderabad is a short hop from Bangalore. One-day corporate shoots, wedding days and campaign shoots all work; travel is inside the all-in quote.

Do you shoot Telugu weddings?

Yes — and the ritual matters. I plan coverage around the muhurtham and the ceremonies that can't be re-staged, so the film and the stills hold both the grandeur and the tradition.

Can you film at tech campuses?

Yes — campus access, badge protocols and NDA work are routine. Leadership films, culture films and event coverage for the Gachibowli–Hitec City corridor are a regular part of my corporate work.
