Most real estate photography in Bangalore is a race to the bottom — ultra-wide, over-bright, lens-distorted frames that make every flat look like every other flat. They fill the portal slot, but they don't sell the space. I shoot property the way a cinematographer frames a scene: correct verticals, real light, and frames that give a home a sense of place.

That matters most where the stakes are highest — builders launching a project, agents marketing premium villas and apartments, and owners listing on portals who are competing with a thousand other Whitefield or Sarjapur listings on the same platform. A cinematic walkthrough film is often the difference between a scroll and a site visit.
