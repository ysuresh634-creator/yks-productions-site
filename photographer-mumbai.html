Common questions

Are you based in Mumbai?

My bases are Bangalore and Dubai, and I'm in Mumbai constantly — for fashion, D2C and film work it's my busiest shoot city. Travel is built into the all-in quote, so a Mumbai booking works exactly like hiring a local.

Do you shoot fashion and product campaigns?

Yes — that's the core of what I do in Mumbai. Lookbooks, editorial, model portfolios and D2C product campaigns, shot for the feed and for print, with video reels from the same set.

Can you shoot photo and video on the same day?

Yes. I'm a cinematographer and a stills photographer, so one booking covers the campaign film, the reels and the stills — one lighting setup, one consistent look, usually cheaper than two separate crews.
