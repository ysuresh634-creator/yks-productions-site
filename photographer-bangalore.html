03

The Bangalore advantage

Bangalore’s mild climate and soft overcast light are kind to outdoor shoots for much of the year, and the city has plenty of character locations: Cubbon Park, old bungalows in Basavanagudi and Malleshwaram, and modern glass offices. I scout locations to match the brief rather than defaulting to a studio.

Product and e-commerce photography in Bangalore

Bangalore’s D2C scene needs consistent catalogue imagery more than almost anything else. I set up a repeatable lighting plan so every SKU matches, shoot to marketplace specs for Amazon and Flipkart, and add lifestyle images in real Bangalore homes, cafes and offices for social and ads.
