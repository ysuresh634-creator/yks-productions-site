01

Standing out in India’s media capital

Mumbai is where Indian advertising, film and fashion meet. Audiences here have seen it all, so brands compete on taste and storytelling as much as budget. The city’s strongest categories (fashion and beauty, D2C consumer brands, BFSI and fintech, hospitality and real estate) all depend on sharp creative.

My experience on Malayalam feature film sets (Soothravakyam, Baby Girl) and brand work for Vaseline (Unilever) and Marriott translates well to Mumbai’s expectations: cinematic content, a clear brand idea and campaigns built around them.

02

What Mumbai brands ask for

Fashion and beauty: lookbooks, reels, creator collaborations and Meta ads. D2C: a steady flow of ad creative and UGC to keep Meta campaigns fresh. Finance and fintech: brand trust content, explainer videos and LinkedIn for leadership. Hospitality and real estate: films and campaigns for launches in areas like Bandra, Worli and Powai.

03

Creator and influencer marketing in Mumbai

Mumbai has India’s densest creator ecosystem. I help brands brief creators clearly, choose them on audience fit rather than follower count, secure the usage rights needed to reuse content in ads, and measure results with codes and links.

04

Working in Mumbai

Shoot days in Bandra, Juhu, Andheri, Lower Parel, BKC, Colaba, Worli and Powai, planned around travel. Strategy, ads and reporting run remotely.

05

Getting started in Mumbai

We start with a remote discovery session and a review of your brand, channels and competitors. Shoot days are then batched into a Mumbai trip so one visit produces as much content as possible: lookbook images, reels, UGC and founder video. Festive seasons like Diwali and the wedding season are planned well ahead, since every brand in the city competes for attention then.
