03

Why it works

Food is one of the hardest things to photograph well — it wilts, it cools, the light turns it grey, and a great dish can look sad in thirty seconds. The whole job is light and speed, and that is exactly what a film background trains. I shape light so texture and freshness read true, work fast so the food is still perfect, and direct plating with your chef so every shot looks like the best version of that dish.

Food photography questions

What food photography do you shoot in Bangalore?

Full menu shoots for restaurants, cafes and cloud kitchens, hero dishes for marketing, delivery-app imagery, drinks and dessert, plus social-first food reels. Everything is shot so the dish looks as good as it tastes.

Do you shoot at our restaurant in Bangalore?

Yes. I shoot on location at your restaurant or cafe with portable lighting, usually before service or during a quiet window so it never disrupts the kitchen. I can also shoot in a hired space for packaged or product food brands.

Do you do food styling?

I handle light styling and plating direction on set with your chef so each dish looks its best on camera. For large campaigns needing a dedicated food stylist, I can bring one in.

Do you shoot food reels and video too?

Yes. The pour, the cheese pull, the steam: short food reels are some of the highest-performing content a restaurant can post. I shoot stills and reels in the same session for one consistent look.

How fast is delivery, and what does it cost?

Edited images are typically delivered within 2 to 3 days, the same turnaround I use for restaurant work in Dubai. Pricing is based on the number of dishes and deliverables: per-dish for small menus or a flat session rate for a full menu. Send your menu size and I will quote.
