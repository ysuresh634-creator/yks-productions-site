Why it works

The hard part of a headshot isn't the camera — it's getting a busy, slightly self-conscious professional to relax in ninety seconds. That's a directing skill, and it's where a film background shows: I direct posture, angle and expression so people who "hate photos" come out looking like the best, most confident version of themselves. Same light and crop across the team means it all matches — one company, one standard.
