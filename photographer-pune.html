Shooting across Pune

City, campus and the hills around.

Koregaon Park and Kalyani Nagar, Baner–Balewadi, Hinjawadi, Magarpatta and Kharadi, old Pune around Shaniwar Wada, and out to Lonavala, Lavasa and Mulshi for resort weddings and shoots. Mumbai is two hours away — combined shoots across both cities work well.

Common questions

Do you cover Pune and Mumbai together?

Yes — they pair well. A campaign can shoot Pune one day and Mumbai the next with the same look; travel between the two is trivial and quoted inside the same all-in number.

Do you shoot resort weddings around Pune?

Constantly — Lonavala, Lavasa, Mulshi and the hill venues. I plan around the landscape and the light rather than fighting them; the hills do half the art direction.

Can a startup afford this?

Talk to me. I quote all-in per project, and young companies are a deliberate part of my client mix — a founder story and a set of campaign assets can be scoped to a startup budget without looking like one.

How do I get a quote?

WhatsApp or the enquiry form with the shoot type, date and venue — you'll get availability and one number that includes shoot, edit, grade, delivery and travel.
