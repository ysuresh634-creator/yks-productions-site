Common questions

Do you shoot traditional Tamil weddings?

Yes — and the muhurtham drives the plan. I map coverage to the ceremonies that can't repeat, shoot candid rather than staged, and deliver stills plus a cinematic film that holds the silk, the smoke and the sea light.

Can you shoot at ECR beach venues?

Yes — ECR and Mahabalipuram venues are regulars. Coastal light is a gift if you know how to use it; I plan the day's schedule around it.

Do you cover corporate and industrial shoots?

Yes — auto, manufacturing and the OMR tech belt. Plant films, leadership stories and event coverage, with safety and access protocols handled properly.
