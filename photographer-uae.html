Common questions

Do you cover all seven emirates?

Yes. I'm based in Dubai, and everything from Abu Dhabi to Ras Al Khaimah is a drive — no flights, no travel drama, one all-in quote.

Do you shoot desert and outdoor locations?

Constantly — desert shoots are a UAE signature. Golden hour in the dunes is planned to the minute, with permits and access handled where venues need them.

Can you do photo and video in one booking?

Yes — that's the default. A wedding's stills and film, a property's photos and walkthrough, a brand's campaign and reels — one eye, one consistent look, usually cheaper than two vendors.

Do you also work in India?

Yes — I'm based between Dubai and Bangalore and shoot across all of India, so UAE brands shooting Indian campaigns (or couples with weddings in both countries) keep one photographer and one look across both.
