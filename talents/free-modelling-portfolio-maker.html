01

What this modelling portfolio online maker is

I’m Yedukrishna Suresh. I photograph fashion and films under YKS Productions, and I run YKS Talents — a free, curated portfolio platform where brands look for models, influencers, creators and actors. I built the maker because too many people were being asked to pay just to put a book together.

This is a free modelling portfolio maker that lives inside the application, not a separate app. You do not pick fonts or drag boxes around. You answer the form honestly, add five to twelve photos, and the builder designs the book from that: a cover from your first close-up, your measurements, the note you wrote (cleaned of any contact details), and your pictures on their own pages.

The layout is generated for you — type, colours and cover — so two applicants do not get an identical-looking file. Photos stay the hero: most pages hold one frame, never more than two (apart from a small strip on the last page). The book is A4, made on your own phone, and a copy travels with your application so I can look at it.

It is a modelling portfolio online and free to use. It is not a public page that publishes your number, and it is not a listing until I have read the application and decided it belongs in the pool.

02

How the free portfolio maker works

  1. Open the application and follow @yks_photoworks. That follow unlocks the submit button. The builder does not run anywhere else.
  2. Fill in the form. Name, phone, email, Instagram, whether you are applying as a model, influencer/creator or actor, date of birth (18 to 34), where you live now and where you are from, education, height, bust, waist and hips, and a short note about yourself in English letters (120 to 600 characters). Shoe, hair and eye colour are optional.
  3. Add your photos. Five are required: two face close-ups, a full-length (head to toe), a mid-shot (waist up) and one more. You can add extras up to twelve. Each picture must be different, and at least 800 pixels on the short side.
  4. Submit. The maker builds the PDF on your phone from what you entered. If that step fails, your application is still sent — a broken PDF never blocks you.
  5. Send the WhatsApp that appears next, then download your portfolio. The message already carries your name, Instagram, city and a link to the book.
  6. I read it. If it is a fit, your profile may be added to the pool brands browse. That is a maybe, not a yes.

03

Photos that belong in a portfolio

A clear picture taken on a phone can work if it is sharp, well-lit and shows you properly. Blurry mobile selfies, bathroom mirrors, group photos, screenshots and anything run through a beauty filter are not added to the talent pool.

  • Two close-ups — your face clearly visible, no sunglasses or mask, in even light.
  • One full-length — head to toe, so a client can see proportion and posture.
  • One mid-shot — roughly waist up.
  • One more — another angle or a simple second look. Then extras only if they show something new, up to twelve in total.

Stand in daylight near a window or in open shade, against a plain wall if you can. Use the rear camera. Turn beauty mode off. Just you in the frame. The form will also reject the same photo dropped into two slots. More on how to shoot a clean set is in the model digitals guide.

Questions about the maker

Is the modelling portfolio maker really free?

Yes. Applying to YKS Talents and using the portfolio builder is free — no registration fee, no portfolio fee, no listing fee and no paid tier. If anyone asks you for money in the name of YKS Talents, it is not me.

Is this a model portfolio maker online, like an editor?

It is a model portfolio maker online, but it is not a drag-and-drop editor and not a blank template. You fill the YKS Talents application with your details, measurements and photos. The site designs a multi-page PDF on your phone from what you submitted.

Do I download the portfolio straight away?

After you submit, there is one last step: send me a WhatsApp (it already has your name, Instagram, city and portfolio link). That unlocks the download of your PDF. The file is yours to keep, whether or not your profile is added to the platform.

Will my profile appear in the talent pool?

Maybe. Every application is read by me personally. If it is a fit, your profile may be added to the pool that brands browse. Signing up does not entitle you to work or guarantee selection. Mobile or poor-quality photos are not added.

Do I need professional photos?

No studio package is required. The form accepts phone photos if they are clear, well-lit and sharp enough (at least 800 pixels on the short side). Blurry mobile selfies, group shots, heavy filters and poor-quality pictures are not added to the talent pool. See the model digitals guide for how to shoot them.

Does this get me paid modelling work?

No. There is nothing called a free lunch here. Paid shoots require experience and a strong portfolio. YKS Talents is not a jobs board and not an agency. An optional portfolio photography session with YKS Productions is a separate service and does not guarantee bookings.

Who can use the free modelling portfolio maker?

Models, influencers, content creators and actors aged 18 to 34, in India or the Middle East. YKS mainly does Western fashion shoots; saree shoots are rare; I do not offer hand modelling.

Are my phone number and Instagram printed on the PDF?

No. The book never prints your phone number, email, WhatsApp or Instagram. Clients who find you on YKS Talents contact me first.
