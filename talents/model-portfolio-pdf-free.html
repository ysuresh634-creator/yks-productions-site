01

What you actually download

I’m Yedukrishna Suresh. The file you get is a multi-page A4 PDF — a small book, not a single flyer. It is designed on your phone when you submit the YKS Talents application, using the photos and details you typed in.

A typical book has:

  • A cover built around your first face close-up, with your name.
  • Your photographs on their own pages. Most pages hold one picture; a page never holds more than two, except a small strip on the back.
  • Your measurements — height, bust, waist, hips, and shoe, hair or eye colour if you added them — plus a cleaned version of the short note you wrote about yourself.
  • A footer that says YKS Talents. It does not print your phone number, email, WhatsApp or Instagram. Clients reach you through me.

People often ask for a “model portfolio template” because they want something ready, not a blank InDesign file. This PDF is ready in that sense — it is already designed — but you cannot open it as a template and swap in new pictures later. If your look changes, you apply again with new photos.

02

How the free download works

  1. Go to the application and follow @yks_photoworks so submit unlocks.
  2. Enter your details, measurements and a short note in English, then add five to twelve photos (two close-ups, full-length, mid-shot, and more).
  3. Submit. The modelling portfolio PDF is generated on your device. A copy is sent with the application so I can review the same book you will download.
  4. Send the WhatsApp that appears — last step, already filled in. That unlocks “Download your portfolio”.
  5. Save the PDF. It is yours to keep and to share. Being added to the public talent pool is separate, and not guaranteed.

If the PDF fails to build for any reason, the application is still sent. You will not lose your place in the queue over a technical hiccup.

03

Photos that make a usable PDF

The book can only be as clear as the pictures you give it. A sharp phone photo in good daylight is fine. A blurry mobile selfie is not, and poor-quality photos are not added to the talent pool even if a PDF still generates.

  • Close-ups: face to camera, even light, no sunglasses.
  • Full-length: head to toe, plain clothes, room around you so nothing is cropped at the ankles.
  • Mid-shot: waist up.
  • At least 800 pixels on the short side; JPG, PNG or HEIC; five different frames minimum, twelve maximum.

Questions about the PDF

Can I download a model portfolio PDF for free?

Yes. After you submit the free YKS Talents application and send the WhatsApp that follows, you can download the designed model portfolio PDF. There is no fee for the file.

Is this a model portfolio template I fill in myself?

No. It is not a blank model portfolio template, not a Canva file and not a Photoshop PSD. The builder designs the modelling portfolio PDF from the photos, measurements and short note you submit on the application.

What is inside the modelling portfolio PDF?

A multi-page A4 book: a cover using your first close-up, pages for your photos (usually one picture per page, never more than two), and your details — height, bust, waist, hips, and optional shoe, hair and eye colour — plus a cleaned version of the note you wrote. Your phone number, email, WhatsApp and Instagram are never printed in the book.

Can I use the PDF outside YKS Talents?

Yes. Once you download it, the PDF is yours to keep and to send where you choose. Whether your profile is added to the YKS Talents pool is a separate decision, made after I read the application.

Why do I have to send a WhatsApp before the download unlocks?

The application has a last step after submit: send me a WhatsApp so I know the application is real and I have a way to reach you. The message is pre-filled with your name, Instagram, city and portfolio link. After that tap, the download button unlocks.

Will poor photos still give me a PDF?

The builder will still try to lay out whatever you uploaded, as long as the files pass the form checks (different photos, at least 800 pixels on the short side). A downloadable PDF is not the same as being added to the talent pool. Mobile or poor-quality photos are not added to the pool.

Does a free PDF mean I will get paid shoots?

No. Applying and the builder are free; paid work is not. Paid shoots require experience and a strong portfolio. Signing up does not entitle you to work or guarantee selection.
