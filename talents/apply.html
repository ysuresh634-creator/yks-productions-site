Before you apply — the honest answers

Do I have to pay anything to join?

No. Joining costs nothing, now or ever — no signing fee, no portfolio fee, no registration charge, no cut of your first booking. If anyone in this industry asks you for money to be represented, walk away.

Do I need professional photos to apply?

No. Phone photos are fine — a clear face shot and a full-length in natural light is enough. If you're right for the roster I'll shoot you properly myself.

Do I need experience?

No. I cast for how someone photographs and how they take direction, not for how long their CV is.

Will my phone number or Instagram be public?

Never. Your contact details are never published on the site and never passed to a client.

Does joining guarantee me work?

No, and anyone who promises otherwise is lying to you. Being on the roster means you're in front of me when a brief comes in that fits you.

Who can apply?

Applicants 18 or over. The roster is curated, so not every application is added, and applications come in through this form only. If you’re a brand looking to book talent, that’s the casting page.

What happens after I apply?

Every submission comes to me, not to a bot. If you fit the roster you hear back from me personally.

Is this a modelling agency?

No. I'm a photographer and director who casts talent for the productions I shoot. There's no representation contract — you stay free to work with anyone else.
