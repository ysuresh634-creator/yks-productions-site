Before you apply — the honest answers

Do I have to pay anything to join?

No. YKS Talents is 100% free — no registration fee, no portfolio fee, no listing fee, no paid tiers and no commission, now or ever. If anyone in this industry asks you for money to be registered, walk away. More on that in modelling agency fees and scams.

Do I need professional photos to apply?

No. Phone photos are fine. You need minimum 5 photos, including 2 clear face close-ups, plus a full-length, a mid-shot and one more, in good light with no heavy filters. You can add up to 12 photos in total. Here’s how to shoot good digitals on your phone.

Do I need experience?

No. I look at how someone photographs and how they take direction, not at how long their CV is. New to this? Start with how to make a modelling portfolio.

Is there a model registration fee?

No. There is no registration fee, joining fee, portfolio fee or listing fee on YKS Talents, now or later. Applying is 100% free, and the portfolio builder is free. If anyone asks you for money in the name of YKS Talents, it is not me. My guide to how to become a model in India explains how to start without paying anyone.

Will my phone number or Instagram be public?

Never. Your contact details are never published on the site and never passed on without your say. Clients who find you on YKS Talents contact me first.

Is YKS Talents a jobs board?

No. YKS Talents doesn’t offer or promise jobs, and anyone who promises guaranteed work is not being honest with you. It’s a 100% free portfolio platform that makes you visible to brands and clients looking for talent. When a client wants to book you, the booking is arranged through YKS.

Who can apply?

Models, influencers, content creators and actors aged 18 to 34, anywhere in India or the Middle East. The platform is curated, so not every application is added, and applications come in through this form only. If you’re a client looking for talent, start at YKS Talents.

I live outside India. Can I still apply?

Yes. In “Where you live”, choose your country or pick “Other country” and type it in, then add your state or region and town, and do the same for your home country. Give your phone number with the country code and write everything in English. The portfolio is free, but it isn’t a job offer, and YKS Talents doesn’t help with visas. See foreign models in India.

I’m not in Mumbai or Bangalore — is it worth applying?

Yes. YKS Talents is open across India and the Middle East, and clients look for talent in cities everywhere.

What happens after I apply?

Every submission comes to me, not to a bot. If your portfolio is a fit for the platform you hear back from me personally. If it isn’t right now, I’d rather tell you that than leave you waiting.

Is this a modelling agency?

No. YKS Talents is a 100% free portfolio platform, not an agency and not a jobs board. There’s no representation contract and nothing you get signed into — you stay free to work with anyone else.
