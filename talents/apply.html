Before you send anything to anyone

How to tell a real casting from a fake one

Plenty of people in this industry charge models to be represented. We don't, and we never will — but you have no reason to take our word for that. So here is the test you can run on any agency, any photographer, any casting call. Including this one.

The one rule

On a real job, the money moves toward you

If someone asks you to pay — a registration fee, a membership tier, a portfolio package, a comp card they'll build for you, a shoot you have to fund — that is the tell, however professional everything around it looks.

We will never ask you for money. Not to join, not to stay on the roster, not to be put forward for a job. If anyone ever asks you for a payment in our name, it isn't us.

You also don't need paid photographs to apply. Clear pictures taken on a phone in daylight are what we ask for first, because that is what a casting director actually wants to see.

The four questions

What a genuine brief tells you up front

Before you agree to anything, a real casting brief answers four things without you having to chase them:

  1. Who the client is — the actual brand, not "a major label".
  2. The shoot date — a date, not "sometime next month".
  3. The location — an address or a city and a studio, not "we'll confirm".
  4. The usage — where the images will appear, and for how long.

Ask for all four. If any of them stays vague after you've asked, walk away. That is true of us as much as anyone — hold us to it.

If you've already paid someone and think it was a scam, the government portal is cybercrime.gov.in, run by the Ministry of Home Affairs. Reporting financial fraud there needs you to register an account first — only the women-and-children category can be filed anonymously.

The bar

What actually gets you on the board

I’m not looking for a particular look — that changes with every brief. I’m looking for someone a client can book on a Tuesday and not think about again. It’s a shorter list than people expect, and almost none of it is about your best photograph.

Photographs, not glamour shots

Daylight, plain wall, hair off your face, clothes that show your actual shape. One full-length, one head and shoulders, one that looks like you on a normal day. No filters, no heavy retouching, no salon hair and makeup — a client is booking the person, and a retouched picture tells me nothing about her.

You answer, and you turn up

Most people don’t lose the job at the casting. They lose it by going quiet for four days, or arriving at eleven for a nine o’clock call. If you pick up your phone and you’re on time, you are already ahead of most of this industry — I’m not exaggerating.

You know what you won’t do

Tell me your lines before a client ever asks. Nobody minds a clear no; it’s the yes-to-everything that becomes a no on the morning of the shoot, and that’s what costs people work. Written down here, I can make sure you’re never put in a room you didn’t want to be in.

And what this isn’t: a free portfolio service. The board exists to put people in front of paying clients — the builder further down is a by-product, made so I’d have something decent to read. If the PDF is what you came for, take it and go, genuinely no hard feelings. If you want to be sent for jobs, fill the form in properly. Half-finished ones don’t get put forward, and that is the only filter here.

Before you apply — the honest answers

The questions I get most, including the ones people are embarrassed to ask.

Do I have to pay anything to join?

No. Joining costs nothing, now or ever — no signing fee, no portfolio fee, no registration charge, no cut of your first booking. If anyone in this industry asks you for money to be represented, walk away. That is the most common scam there is, and it is the reason I say this first.

Do I need professional photos to apply?

No. Phone photos are fine — a clear face shot and a full-length in natural light is enough to see what I need to see. If you're right for the roster I'll shoot you properly myself.

Do I need experience?

No. I cast for how someone photographs and how they take direction, not for how long their CV is. Plenty of the best faces on a set that day had never done a paid job before.

Will my phone number or Instagram be public?

Never. Your contact details are never published on the site and never passed to a client. Brands enquire through me, I check the booking is real and the dates work, and only then do we talk about you being on it.

Does joining guarantee me work?

No, and anyone who promises otherwise is lying to you. Being on the roster means you're in front of me when a brief comes in that fits you. Some months that's several briefs; some months it's none. I won't pretend otherwise to get your application.

Who can apply?

Anyone 18 or over, anywhere in India. Models, actors and creators. The roster is 18+ only — casting a child artist in India needs prior written permission from the District Magistrate under the Child Labour (Prohibition and Regulation) Act, and that isn't something I take on.

I'm not in Bangalore or Mumbai — is it worth applying?

Yes. The roster is pan-India, and briefs come in for cities all over the country. Where you are matters for a specific job, not for whether you belong on the list.

What happens after I apply?

Every submission comes to me, not to a bot. If you fit the roster you hear back from me personally. If you don't fit right now I'd rather tell you that than leave you waiting.

Is this a modelling agency?

No. I'm a photographer and director who casts talent for the productions I shoot. There's no representation contract and nothing you get signed into — you stay free to work with anyone else, always.
