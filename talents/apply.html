Before you apply — the honest answers

Do I have to pay anything to join?

No. Joining costs nothing, now or ever — no joining fee, no registration fee, no portfolio fee, no paid tiers. If anyone in this industry asks you for money to be registered, walk away. More on that in modelling agency fees & scams.

Do I need professional photos to apply?

No. Phone photos are fine — a clear face shot and a full-length in natural light is enough. Here’s how to shoot good digitals on your phone.

Do I need experience?

No. I look at how someone photographs and how they take direction, not at how long their CV is. New to this? Start with how to make a modelling portfolio.

Will my phone number or Instagram be public?

Never. Your contact details are never published on the site. Clients who find you on YKS Talents contact me first.

Is YKS Talents a jobs board?

No. YKS Talents doesn’t offer or promise jobs. It’s a free portfolio platform where clients find talent — and where I cast my own YKS shoots from.

Who can apply?

Women aged 18 to 34, anywhere in India or the Middle East. The platform is curated, so not every application is added, and applications come in through this form only. If you’re a client looking for talent, start at YKS Talents.

What happens after I apply?

Every submission comes to me, not to a bot. If your portfolio is a fit for the platform you hear back from me personally.

Is this a modelling agency?

No. YKS Talents is a free portfolio platform, not an agency and not a jobs board. There’s no representation contract — you stay free to work with anyone else.
