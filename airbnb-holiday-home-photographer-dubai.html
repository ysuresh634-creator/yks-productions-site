02

What a holiday-home shoot covers

Listing

Airbnb listing photography

Room-by-room stills with straight verticals and natural colour, so a guest is looking at the space, not at a distortion of it.

Stay

The frames that win a stay

The bed, the view, the kitchen, the balcony, the pool or gym access: the amenities people filter for, photographed so they are easy to believe.

Interiors

Interior photography

Interior work is most of this job. Light is planned around the apartment’s orientation, and furniture is left honest rather than staged into a different home.

Film

Walkthroughs & reels

A short walkthrough and vertical cuts from the same visit, useful when the layout or the view does more of the selling than a still can.

Platforms

Airbnb, Booking.com and your page

Crops and counts planned for the platforms the property actually uses, plus a set you can put on your own site.

Portfolio

Several homes, one look

If you manage more than one holiday home, a consistent look across the portfolio makes the brand feel deliberate. Say how many units are in the brief and I will quote the set.

How a listing shoot works

Brief

Prep

Light is planned around the orientation. Building access is arranged before the day, not at the gate. The home should be guest-ready; I photograph it, I do not restyle it into a different apartment.

Shoot

Listing stills, and a walkthrough or reels when they are in the brief, captured on the same visit.

Step 04

Delivery

Edited photos typically within 24 to 48 hours, the film within 3 to 5 days when it was part of the quote.

Questions

Airbnb photography questions

What does an Airbnb photographer in Dubai shoot?

Listing stills with corrected verticals, room-by-room coverage, exteriors and amenities, and, when the brief asks, a short walkthrough film and vertical reels. The set is built for Airbnb, Booking.com and other short-stay platforms, as well as your own listing page.

How is this different from a hotel or sales listing shoot?

Holiday homes and short-stay apartments need a guest to imagine a stay, not a buyer to imagine owning the property. The shot list leans on the bed, the light, the view, the kitchen and the amenities that show up in a search filter. A full hotel set, with every room category and trade spaces, lives on the hotel photographer in Dubai page. A sales listing lives on the real estate photographer in Dubai page.

How fast is delivery?

Edited photos are typically delivered in 24 to 48 hours, and a walkthrough film in 3 to 5 days when it is part of the brief. Same-day rush is available when a listing has to go live quickly. Those are the same turnaround times I use for other Dubai property stills.

Do you shoot stills and video on the same visit?

Yes. Once a room is lit, I can shoot the stills and a short walkthrough or reels from the same setup, so the listing and the film look like the same home.

How much does Airbnb photography in Dubai cost?

I quote one all-in number after I know the size of the property, whether you need a walkthrough film as well as stills, and how quickly the set has to be live. There is no public rate card.
