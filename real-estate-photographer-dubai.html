The aerial edge

Here's something most Dubai listings get wrong: the drone footage is flown illegally. Commercial aerial in Dubai requires DCAA approval and a GCAA remote pilot licence — and shooting without them risks heavy fines and footage you can't legally use. I hold the relevant approvals, so the aerial of your property is fully licensed, insured and usable. When a developer needs the Palm, the Marina or a Downtown rooftop framed from the air, it's covered — legally — in the same shoot.
