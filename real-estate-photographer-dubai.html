Choosing well

What a Dubai listing set should include

Corrected stills, honest light, and a film if the property has to compete on the portal.

On Property Finder or Bayut, a Marina or Downtown apartment is scrolling next to dozens that look just like it. The set has to do two jobs: show the rooms accurately (straight verticals, real colour, no ultra-wide stretch) and give a sense of what it feels like to walk through. That is why I shoot stills and a walkthrough film on the same visit whenever the brief allows.

A typical listing set covers the hero exterior or view, living spaces, kitchen, bedrooms, bathrooms, balcony or terrace, amenities if they belong to the unit, and twilight exteriors when the building warrants them. Holiday homes and hotel apartments sit next to this work; a full hotel recategorisation is a different brief and lives on the hotel photographer in Dubai page.

Property

Apartment, villa or show unit

A one-bed, a Palm villa and an off-plan show unit are different days. Size and styling time move the quote more than the postcode does.
