How much does it cost to hire a model in India?

There isn’t a single rate, and anyone quoting one without asking about your usage is guessing. Fees are agreed per project. These are the things that move the number most:

Usage term

How long the images run. Short social use is at one end; long campaign terms, or perpetual use, at the other.

Territory

India only, the Gulf, or worldwide. Wider territory usually costs more.

Channels

Organic social, paid ads, e-commerce, print, outdoor or packaging. The harder the images work, the higher the fee.

Shoot length

Hours or days on set, number of looks, and any fittings beforehand.

Exclusivity

Whether the talent can work with a competitor during your term. Most shoots don’t need it; if yours does, it has to be paid for.

Travel

Out-of-city shoots add travel and stay. Agree who covers what up front.

Browsing YKS Talents and sending a brief cost nothing. Talent never pay to be on the platform.

Releases & usage — sorted before the shoot

You’re not just paying for photographs; you’re paying for permission to use someone’s face for a defined purpose, in defined places, for a defined time. Put the term, territory, channels and any exclusivity in writing, with a signed model release, before anyone arrives on set. It’s the step most shoots skip and regret when a campaign wants a renewal.

When I’m shooting, I handle that paperwork as part of the production. If you work with the talent directly, I’d still strongly recommend it — it protects both of you.

Finding a model — the questions I get

How do I find models for a photoshoot in India?

Browse the portfolios on YKS Talents, or send a short brief on this page — the look, the city, the dates and what the images are for. I come back with suitable profiles from the platform, usually within a day. From there you can deal with the talent directly once she has agreed, or keep it running through the brief.

How do I hire a model for a photoshoot?

Start with a clear brief: the look, the city, the dates, the usage (where and for how long the images will run) and your budget range. Shortlist from portfolios, check availability, agree the fee and usage in writing, and sign a model release before the shoot.

How much does it cost to hire a model in India?

There isn’t one rate. Model fees are agreed per project and depend mostly on usage — how long the images run, where, and on which channels — plus shoot length, travel and any exclusivity. A one-day social content shoot and a long outdoor campaign can use the same face and cost very differently. Browsing YKS Talents and sending a brief are free.
