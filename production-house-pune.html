Why Pune teams call me

Corporate video that doesn’t look corporate

Pune’s economy is unusually varied: IT and services in Hinjewadi, Kharadi and Magarpatta; automotive and engineering around Chakan and Pimpri-Chinchwad; one of India’s biggest concentrations of colleges and universities; and a steady pipeline of residential projects. That makes Pune one of the strongest markets for corporate video production: profile films, capability films, campus films and launch videos.

My job is to make that work feel human. Real people interviewed well, b-roll that shows what actually happens in your building, light that flatters a factory floor or a lecture hall, and an edit with a clear story. You get a master film plus the cut-downs your HR, sales and social teams need.

Because Pune is close to Mumbai, a lot of my Pune work is planned alongside Mumbai shoots, which keeps travel efficient for both.

Locations

Shooting in Pune

Pune offers old and new within a short drive: the wadas and lanes of the old Peth areas, leafy Koregaon Park and the Camp area, modern IT campuses in Hinjewadi and Kharadi, and the Sahyadri ghats just outside the city, which turn green and dramatic after the monsoon. Campuses and plants usually need visitor passes and photography approvals, which I arrange through your facilities team during planning.

The monsoon (roughly June to September) is beautiful on camera but unpredictable, so I schedule exteriors with a weather fallback.

Who you’re hiring

One person, start to finish

I want to be straight about this, because most production-house websites aren’t. YKS Productions is me, Yedukrishna Suresh. There is no office, no account manager and no junior who turns up on the day instead of the person you briefed. I write the treatment, direct, shoot and edit.

When a brief genuinely needs more hands (a gaffer for a big lighting set-up, a sound recordist for interviews, a stylist or a make-up artist), I bring in freelancers for that project only. They report to me, they’re in the quote from the start, and you still deal with one person from first message to final file. If the shoot needs faces, I can cast them through YKS Talents.

What that means for a brand in Pune: one conversation instead of a chain of hand-offs, decisions made on set by the person who will edit the footage, and a quote that is one all-in number rather than a rate card with extras.

How it works

From brief to final cut

1

Brief

Send the brief on WhatsApp or through the quote form: what you are launching, who it is for, where it will run, and the date you need it by.

2

Treatment & quote

I come back with an approach (look, references, shot list outline) and one all-in number covering shoot, edit and any freelancers the brief needs.

3

Pre-production

Locations, permissions, casting, props and a call sheet. You approve the plan before anyone is booked, so the shoot day has no surprises.

4

Shoot

Sony FX3 for video, Sony A7 IV for stills, real lighting and a gimbal. I direct and operate the camera myself, so what we agree is what gets shot.

5

Edit, grade, deliver

I edit and colour-grade what I shoot, then deliver every cut and aspect ratio you need for web, social and ads, with revisions as agreed in the quote.

FAQ

Questions brands ask first

Do you offer corporate video production in Pune?

Yes: profile films, capability films, culture and hiring videos, campus films and event recaps. Travel from Bangalore is included in the all-in quote.

Can you film inside our plant?

Yes, within your safety and confidentiality rules. Share the restrictions at the brief stage and we plan the shot list around them.

Can you make admissions content for a college?

Yes. A campus film plus a set of vertical reels from the same shoot covers most admissions campaigns.

Are you a production house or a freelancer?

An independent production house run by one person, me. I bring in freelancers per project only when a brief needs them.

How much does it cost?

Every project is quoted as one all-in number once I know the brief: number of shoot days, locations, cast, and how many cuts you need. There is no public rate card because two “brand films” can be completely different jobs. The fastest route is the 60-second quote form or a WhatsApp message.

Do you own the footage, or do we?

You get full usage rights to the delivered films and photos for your brand, including paid ads, as set out in the quote. If a cast member’s usage needs to be limited (for example to a period or a market), that is agreed up front.

Can you edit footage we already shot?

No. I edit what I shoot, because the edit is planned on set. I’m not an editor-for-hire for other people’s footage.
