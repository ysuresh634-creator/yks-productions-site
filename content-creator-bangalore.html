Choosing well

What to look for in a content creator in Bangalore

The feed and the ad account are different jobs. The useful person can do both.

A content creator in Bangalore is often hired for one of two reasons: a month of feed content the founder is tired of making, or UGC-style ads that do not look like ads. I plan both from the same shoot day when I can, so a product, a cafe, a clinic or a D2C brand walks away with reels, stills and ad cuts rather than a single hero clip.

Ask who will actually shoot (it will be me), whether you own the files and can run paid ads with them (yes), and whether the work is planned around hooks and platforms or filmed first and figured out later. I come from a film background, so light, motion and pacing are controlled on the day. A campaign reel of mine has crossed 3.3M views; that is the standard I hold the work to, not a promise about your next post.
