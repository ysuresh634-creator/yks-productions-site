Privacy and personalisation

I’m Yedukrishna Suresh, and this is my site. It adapts to you a little, the way a good shop remembers what you were looking at, but it does that on your own device and you can wipe it in one tap.

What the site remembers, and where

To show you the right work and the right next step, the site keeps a small note in your browser’s own storage (localStorage). It stays on your device, with the one exception noted below.

  • What you looked at: which pages you opened, roughly how long you stayed and how far you scrolled, and what you typed into the site search.
  • Your device and visit: phone, tablet or desktop, whether your connection is slow or in data-saver mode, the time of day on your device, whether you have been here before, and how you arrived (for example from Instagram, Google, LinkedIn or a WhatsApp link).
  • Your region: your device’s timezone and language settings, so visitors in India see my India number and visitors in the UAE and the Gulf see my UAE number. No IP lookup, no GPS.
  • How you first found the site (for example Google or Instagram), kept for 90 days. This is the one item that can leave your device: when you send an enquiry or a WhatsApp message, a short “Found via” line is added so I know which channel works. You can see it before you press send on WhatsApp.
  • Form details you typed before: your name, phone and email from an enquiry form, so you don’t have to type them again. Only on this device.

What it changes

  • “Picked for you” and “You may also like” suggestions, and a “Recently viewed” list.
  • The quick contact button: tap-to-WhatsApp on a phone, the 60-second quote form on a computer.
  • One “Before you go” note at most per visit, which you can close.
  • On a slow connection, video waits until you tap it.

What it never does

  • It never uses your microphone or camera.
  • No fingerprinting, no tracking you across other sites, no ad trackers.
  • No fake countdowns, fake “only 2 left” messages or made-up reviews. The brands named on the site are brands I have actually worked with.
  • Search engines and visitors without JavaScript see the same published page as everyone else.

If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, personalisation stays off. If you have asked for reduced motion, the site’s pop-ups appear without animation.

Personalisation needs JavaScript, so with it switched off nothing is stored.

What you choose to send me

  • Enquiry forms go to my own Google account (via Google Apps Script) so I can reply. The page you sent it from comes with it.
  • WhatsApp and phone are your own app; I only see what you send.
  • The chat assistant sends what you type to my server so it can answer.
  • YKS Talents applications are covered by their own terms on the application page.
  • Visit statistics: the site uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are useful.

This notice was written with India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act and the UAE’s Personal Data Protection Law in mind. To see, correct or delete anything you have sent me, email yksproductions893@gmail.com.
