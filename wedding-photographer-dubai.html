Dubai weddings are unlike anywhere else — a multi-day Indian celebration in a five-star ballroom, an intimate expat ceremony on the beach, a desert sundowner, a cross-cultural fusion of traditions. What they share is scale and emotion, and both deserve to be told like a film, not documented like a checklist.

My background is in cinematography and stills for South Indian cinema, and I bring that same eye to a Dubai wedding — candid, unposed, and cinematic. I stay close to the emotion and out of the way, and come back with frames and a film that feel exactly like the day felt: the glance before the vows, the tears at the vidaai, the first dance under the lights.
