Talent Agreement

The short version — the whole deal, in plain words.

This is between me, Yedukrishna (I run YKS Productions here in Bengaluru) and the person who signs below. I’ll just call you the “Talent” through the rest of it. I’ve kept this short and in normal words on purpose. I’d honestly rather you read it and know what you’re agreeing to than sign pages of jargon nobody reads. If we ever land a really big brand contract there’s a longer, detailed version we can both pull out, but for now, this is us.

A quick word on who you’re signing with

YKS Productions is my production house. I shoot brand films, fashion and campaigns, mostly out of Bangalore and Dubai. The talent side is new, and honestly that’s the best part for you. While the roster is still small, you get far more of my attention and far more pitches than you ever would sitting in some big agency’s list of a few hundred names.

Here’s how it works, plainly. You don’t pay me anything. Brands find me through the roster, they pay me, and my job is to keep putting you in front of them. No joining fee, no portfolio fee, no commission, nothing out of your pocket to be represented. (There are one or two optional paid extras, like managing your socials, but only if you actually want them.) If some “agency” has burned you before, this is me doing the opposite. Points 2, 4 and 5 are the ones I’d read twice.

Two ways to work with me, and why exclusive gets you more

Non-exclusive is the default, and it’s the easy yes. You keep everything you already have — your other agencies, your managers, your own work, all of it. You drop nothing. Think of me as an extra stream of work coming in, never a replacement. Nothing is locked, nothing is lost.

Exclusive is the one I’d pick for you. Here your brand work runs through me for the term — so, unlike non-exclusive, you don’t also run brand deals through other agencies while we’re exclusive. In return you go to the front of the queue. When a good brief lands you get first refusal, before anyone else on the roster. You sit featured at the top, where brands look first. Put simply, my best deals and my best brands go to my exclusive faces first. And there’s no trap in it, because it ends the day the agreement ends.

A simple example Say you already work with Agency X.
Non-exclusive: you keep Agency X exactly as it is, and I bring you extra brand work on top. You give up nothing.
Exclusive: your brand deals come only through me for the year — not through Agency X — and in return you’re the first name I send when the best brands come looking.

You pick below. Exclusive just earns you priority. It’s never a condition of being represented, you can still say no to any single job, and it’s never a promise a brand will say yes.

  1. Everything I run for you.
    This isn’t a listing you sit on and wait. From the day you sign, I’m working your name across all of this:
    • Getting you found. A roster profile built and optimised for SEO (search), so when a brand or agency looks for someone in your category, you come up. Please note: you go live on my website straight away, but SEO takes time to spread — being visible globally takes 25–30 days. That is normal search-propagation time, not a delay from my side.
    • Pitching you out, actively. I put your profile in front of the right brands directly and continuously — the right people, the right categories — not sitting back waiting for enquiries.
    • The leads you bring me. Any enquiry landing in your own DMs — hand it to me and I take it over: I negotiate the rate and terms, and I follow up with the client so nothing stalls or goes cold.
    • Holding your rate. I negotiate hard for the best number, protect your floor, and never quote you cheap to close something faster.
    • Filtering the junk. Lowball offers, unsafe shoots, brands that don’t pay — I screen them out before they ever reach you.
    • Running the whole job. Brief, scheduling, call sheets, approvals, revisions, delivery, the contract, the usage terms — all handled so you just turn up and perform.
    • Getting you paid. I raise the invoice, chase the client, and make sure the money actually reaches your account on time — not stuck for weeks.
    • On-set support optional If you ask me well in advance and I’m free, I can be on the shoot with you — making sure the brief is honoured, you’re looked after, and it runs the way it should. It’s there if you want it, never assumed and never a guarantee.
    • Protecting your rights. Brands only use what they paid for, in the media and for the time agreed — I keep watch so your face isn’t running somewhere you never agreed to.
    • Your portfolio & comp card. Built and kept current, so you always look brand-ready when I put you forward.
    • Growth & content strategy paid add-on Want to grow your own following faster? Content plans, hooks that actually hold attention, what to post and when, and real traction — powered by the AI-driven growth system I’ve built. This one is a separate paid service — the cost is discussed and fixed with you upfront — and your free representation never depends on it.
    • Your social media optional If you want it, I’ll run your content, posting and growth too — tick the box in your details below. Leave it unticked and I simply won’t; your representation is exactly the same either way.
  2. You pay me nothing, and here’s the honest reason.
    No joining fee, no monthly fee, no commission, and nothing taken out of what you earn, whether I brought you the work or you brought it in yourself. This isn’t me being generous, it’s just how my business works. Your profile sits out there on my website for the whole world to see, and that roster is how brands find me and come to work with me. They’re the ones who pay me. Your visibility is literally my business. So you tell me the number you want in your hand, that exact number lands in your account, and whatever I manage to get above it is what the brand pays me.
  3. Key termThe fair exchange: what you do for YKS, free.
    Your representation costs you nothing, so here’s what I ask back, and none of it costs you a rupee either:
    • One complimentary shoot with me, no fee either way — including the behind-the-scenes — which I use for my Instagram, website and advertising to promote the roster and pull brands in.
    • Post our shoot as an Instagram “collab” (co-authored) so it appears on your grid and mine — your audience sees YKS, and that shared reach is what keeps this free.
    • Announce that you’ve joined — a story tagging @yks_photoworks when you come on board.
    • Show you’re represented — add “Represented by YKS Productions” (or @yks_photoworks) to your bio or a pinned story highlight while you’re on the roster.
    • Tag YKS at least once a month — one story or post tagging @yks_photoworks while represented, plus credit me on content from our shoots and reshare when I ask (kept reasonable, never spammy).
    • A short testimonial — text or a quick video — I can use, once you’ve seen how I work.
    It’s a fair trade. This is the engine that keeps your representation free, and you walk away with proper professional work and some visibility of your own too. And your boundaries always come first. Nothing intimate, and nothing beyond what you’ve already agreed to.
  4. Read this carefullyI’ll never lie to you. No false promises, ever.
    You’ve probably met the other kind. The “agency” that swears you’ll be a star, promises you work, asks you for money for a “portfolio” or a “registration,” and then goes quiet. That is not me and it never will be. The truth is simple: I can’t make a brand say yes. Nobody can, and anyone who tells you they can is lying to your face. What I can do, and what I do every single day, is put you where brands are looking, pitch you to them again and again, and the moment a yes comes, handle all of it for you: the rate, the shoot, and your money reaching you the same day. I’d much rather tell you the truth and earn your trust than promise you the moon and lose it. This is real visibility, done properly. It was never a guarantee of stardom, because no such guarantee exists.
  5. Key termYour money is yours. I never touch it.
    This is where things go wrong at most agencies, so please read it. You onboard once. After that the brand pays me, and I send your full agreed amount to you the same day the brand’s money reaches me. I don’t hold it overnight, I don’t sit on it for weeks, and I never keep a single rupee of it. I’m honestly not interested in your money. What I earn is whatever the brand pays me, on top and separately. Your money is safe with me, and it reaches your account the same day it reaches mine.The one thing I ask back: if I introduce you to a brand, that brand’s work stays with me. Please don’t go around me to deal with them directly. It still costs you nothing.
  6. You own your content — and you let me use it to work for you.
    Everything you make stays yours. But so I can actually do everything in point 1, you grant me the right to use the photos and videos you share with me, in three specific ways:
    • To pitch you to brands — including through the private AI system I’ve built that puts your profile and work in front of the right brands, automatically and at scale.
    • To share with my clients — showing your work to the brands and agencies I’m in conversation with.
    • To make social-media creatives — content and posts that promote you and the roster.
    • To show you on my website — your photos and work on the YKS roster, so brands searching your category can find you. This is the visibility that gets you seen.
    This licence is only ever to represent and promote you — I never sell your content, and any use by a brand is a separate deal for only what that brand pays for. Nothing intimate, nude or suggestive is ever used, pitched or shot without your separate written yes.
  7. You can always say no.
    Any brief, any brand — decline it, no reason owed, no penalty, any time before you confirm it. Your comfort and your choices come first, always.
  8. You’re safe, and you’re respected — always.
    Every shoot I put you on is a professional, respectful space. You’re welcome to bring someone with you to any shoot. If anything ever feels unsafe, unprofessional or just wrong, you walk. On the spot, no permission needed, and you’re still paid for whatever you’ve already done. And nothing intimate, nude or suggestive is ever pitched, agreed or shot without your own separate, specific written “yes” for that exact job. If a line ever gets crossed, you tell me and I handle it. That part is my job, not yours.
  9. Your details stay private.
    Your phone number, address and bank details never go to a brand without your say-so. Enquiries come to me first.
  10. Key termMy privacy, my identity and YKS Productions — strictly protected.
    My privacy matters as much as yours, and on this there is no grey area. During this agreement and at any time after it ends, you will not — in any form, by any means, on any platform, to any person:
    • use or disclose my name, my photo, image, likeness, voice or signature anywhere, for anything, whether or not it names me;
    • reveal or discuss anything about me or about YKS Productions — my identity, private life, whereabouts, my methods or working model, or the company’s business, clients, dealings or any detail of it — to any client, brand or other person;
    • post any “callout”, accusation, screenshot, chat, tag, mention, review or other content about me or YKS Productions, publicly or privately, online or offline;
    • photograph, film or record me, or take, use or post a photo or video with me, without my explicit written permission each time.
    My name, image, voice and likeness are protected by my personality and publicity rights under Article 21 of the Constitution and Indian personality-rights law; the YKS Productions name and marks under the Trade Marks Act, 1999; the company’s private information as confidential; and any false or damaging public statement is defamation under s.356 of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023. Any breach is a serious breach that ends this agreement immediately, without notice, and entitles me to an injunction under the Specific Relief Act, 1963 and to compensation under s.73 of the Indian Contract Act, 1872. These obligations are permanent and survive termination. This never prevents you from making a genuine complaint to the police, a court or a lawful authority — that right always remains.
  11. Keep this strictly between us.
    This agreement is private and confidential. You will not discuss our working terms with anyone — not your rate, not what I earn, not how we work, not what is in this document — with other talent, other agencies, brands, or in public. You will also not discuss me, my working model, my methods or my principles with a client, a brand, or any person other than me. Doing so is treated as bad conduct and can end this agreement immediately, without prior notice. I hold the exact same confidence about you. This protects both of us, and it continues even after the agreement ends.
  12. Respect, both ways — and where it ends.
    We deal with each other, and with every brand, professionally. Any conduct that insults, embarrasses, defames or brings me, YKS Productions or a brand into disrepute — online or in person — is not expected and not accepted, and if it happens I may end this agreement at my sole discretion, with immediate effect. This runs both ways: I owe you the same respect and professionalism at all times.
  13. Read & sign for thisHow this ends — and what happens to pending money.
    To end this, you send me one formal email giving 5 working days’ notice, from the email address in your details. A WhatsApp, a call, a post or going quiet does not end it. Only the email does, and that way we both hold the same dated record of when it ended. From the day I receive your official email, I stop all pitching, payments and follow-ups for you. If any brand payment is still pending at that point, you collect it directly from that brand yourself — YKS is not responsible for it in any way, and will not chase it or route it for you. No exit fee either way. On exit you come off the roster; you keep everything that is yours (point 6), and privacy (point 9) plus anything already agreed stay in force. This is a personal-services contract, so under s.14 of the Specific Relief Act, 1963 neither of us can be forced to keep working together; the remedy for a breach is money (compensation under s.73 of the Contract Act), never being forced to continue.
