Fashion Photography · Bengaluru

Fashion Photographer in Bangalore

Anyone can photograph a garment. The job is photographing the feeling of wearing it. Editorials, designer lookbooks and the actor portfolios that get callbacks — shot with a film-set eye. I'm Yedukrishna Suresh, founder of YKS Productions, shooting cinematic fashion and portraits across Bengaluru and the South Indian film world.

Book a Bangalore shoot See fashion work

Bengaluru is quietly one of India's most stylish cities — a place of independent designers, startup founders who care how they're seen, and a film industry next door that's always casting. What it doesn't have enough of is fashion photography that feels cinematic rather than commercial. That's the gap I fill.

My background is in cinematography and on-set stills for South Indian cinema — most recently the Malayalam features Soothravakyam and Baby Girl, and portrait work with actors including Rukmini Vasanth and Ashika Ranganath. I bring that same control of light and story to a fashion set in Bangalore, whether it's a designer's new collection, a model building a portfolio, or a founder who wants their personal brand to look like a magazine cover.

Shooting across Bengaluru

The city has more range than people expect — I use all of it.

From the independent studios of Indiranagar and Koramangala for controlled, editorial light, to the greenery of Cubbon Park and Lalbagh, the texture of Church Street and the old cantonment, the glass of UB City, and Nandi Hills at sunrise when a shoot needs scale — Bengaluru gives a fashion set genuine variety. I scout the right backdrop for your brief and arrive knowing how each location reads on camera through the day.
