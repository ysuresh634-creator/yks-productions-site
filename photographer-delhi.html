Common questions

Do you cover Delhi, Gurugram and Noida?

Yes — the whole NCR. Venue, farmhouse, office or studio; travel across the region is built into the quote, and outstation wedding travel is quoted all-in too.

Do you shoot big Indian weddings?

Constantly — 100+ weddings filmed. Multi-day Delhi weddings are their own craft: sangeet to pheras, I cover them with a team sized to the day, and deliver both candid stills and a cinematic film.

Can you handle corporate work under NDA?

Yes. Leadership films, internal events and product launches for NCR corporates often run under NDA — that's routine, and deliverables are matched to your brand guidelines.

How far ahead should I book?

Wedding dates in the Nov–Feb season go months ahead — lock those early. Corporate and brand shoots usually need two to three weeks. Short notice is sometimes possible; ask with the date.
