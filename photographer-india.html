How pan-India works

One quote, travel included, no local-vendor roulette.

Tell me the city, the date and the brief. I quote one all-in number — shoot, edit, grade, delivery and travel — whether that's Jaipur or Coimbatore, Ahmedabad or Guwahati. For multi-city campaigns I keep one look across every location, and for weddings I travel with the couple: haldi in one city, reception in another, one continuous story.

Common questions

Which cities in India do you cover?

All of them. Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bangalore, Hyderabad, Chennai, Pune, Kolkata, Kochi and Goa have dedicated pages here, and everywhere else — Jaipur, Ahmedabad, Lucknow, Indore, Chandigarh, Coimbatore, Guwahati — works exactly the same way: one all-in quote with travel inside.

Doesn't hiring a travelling photographer cost more than a local one?

Usually less than you'd guess, and often less than a premium local studio. I'm one person with a film background, not a crew with overheads — and the quote is all-in, so you compare one honest number against another.

Can you shoot a multi-city campaign with one look?

That's exactly where this model wins. Same eye, same lighting logic, same grade in every city — a brand's Delhi film and Chennai film cut together like one production, because they are.

How do I check your availability?

WhatsApp me the city and the date. I reply the same day with availability and a quote; peak wedding season (Nov–Feb) books months out, brand work needs two to three weeks.
