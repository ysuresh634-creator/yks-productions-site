# Off-page — getting corroborated on domains you don't own

Every claim on this site — the film credits, the clients, the view counts —
exists only here. To a search engine and to an AI assistant, a claim that
appears on exactly one domain, and that domain is your own, is marketing.
It isn't evidence.

That is the whole of the off-page problem. It is not "we need more backlinks."

**The success metric is corroboration, not enquiries.** After each item
below, the question to ask is: *does a substantive fact about me now exist
on a domain I don't control?* Expect zero client enquiries from most of
this. That isn't failure — the citations are what let the ranking and the
AI answers happen later.

All name/phone/description copy comes from `PROFILES.md`. **Paste it
verbatim.** A citation only counts when the details match what the site
says; paraphrasing breaks the match that makes them one entity.

## 2. Ask the clients (the best link available to him)

A credit line on a client's own site outranks every directory on this
page, because it is a third party stating a fact about work he did.

Real, nameable clients: **Reportage Properties**, **Storeys Real Estate**.

Ask for one line on the project or about page, worded plainly:

```
Property films by Yedukrishna Suresh — yksproductions.com
```

Nothing more. No badge, no logo request, no reciprocal link offer. If
they say no, that is the end of it — never imply a credit that wasn't
given.

| **Yellow Pages UAE** | yellowpages.ae | UAE. Use +971 50 195 5122. |

For every one of them, three rules:

- **Name is `YKS Productions`.** Not "YKS Photography", not the handle.
- **Service area, never a street address.** He is a service-area business;
  a made-up address is what gets a listing flagged.
- **The number must match the country** — India listings get the +91,
  UAE listings the +971. A mismatch is exactly the inconsistency that
  stops these counting as one entity.

Category, everywhere it is offered: **Video production service** first,
**Photographer** second — the same order the Business Profiles now use.
