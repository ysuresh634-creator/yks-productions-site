01

Why it matters

Most small-business sites in Dubai lose people in the same places: slow loading on mobile data, a hero image that says nothing, no clear price-or-quote route, and a contact form nobody answers. A site that works does the opposite. It loads fast, shows the real work, answers the obvious questions and makes it one tap to get in touch.

Because I also shoot photo and video, the site and its visuals can be planned together, so you are not left trying to fill a beautiful layout with stock images. And because I run SEO and ads, every page is built with search titles, structured data and conversion tracking from day one, rather than bolted on later.

03

Built for how Dubai customers buy

In Dubai, a big share of enquiries start on WhatsApp and on a phone, often after someone found you on Instagram or Google Maps. A site here should make WhatsApp the main call to action, show your location and service areas clearly, and link properly to your Google Business Profile and social accounts.

Audiences are international, so the design leans on strong visuals and short, plain English that reads well for non-native speakers. If you need an Arabic version, I build the structure for it and work with your translator for the copy.

06

Common questions

What platform do you build on?

It depends on who will update the site. A lightweight hand-built site is fastest and cheapest to run; WordPress or Shopify make sense when your team needs to edit products or posts often. I recommend one in the brief and explain why.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. I start with an audit of what is working, what is slowing it down and what is missing for SEO and enquiries, then rebuild or refresh as needed without losing pages that already rank.

Will my website rank on Google?

A well-built site gives you the foundation: fast loading, clear titles, structured data and pages for each service. Ranking also depends on competition, content and your Google Business Profile, which is why I offer SEO alongside. I won’t promise positions; nobody honest can.

Can you shoot the photos for the site too?

Yes. I’m a photographer and videographer, so the site can launch with real images of your work, space and people rather than stock.

How much does a website cost in Dubai?

It depends on the number of pages, features and whether you need copy, photos or an online store. I send one written quote after the brief, with nothing hidden.
