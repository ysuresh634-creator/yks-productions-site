Why it works

Food is one of the hardest things to photograph well — it wilts, it cools, the light turns it grey, and a great dish can look sad in thirty seconds. The whole job is light and speed, and that's exactly what a film background trains. I shape light so texture and freshness read true, work fast so the food is still perfect, and direct plating with your chef so every shot looks like the best version of that dish. Appetising, accurate, on time.
