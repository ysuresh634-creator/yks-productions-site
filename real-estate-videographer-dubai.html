Real estate video questions

How do I choose the best real estate videographer in Dubai?

Look past camera lists and at the films themselves: are the verticals correct, is the light real, and does the edit make you want to walk the property? The best real estate video is shot by someone with a film background, not just a wide lens — paced, graded and scored so a scroll turns into a viewing. Ask to see a full walkthrough film, not just a 6-second clip.

What does a Dubai property film cost?

It depends on the unit, the location and the deliverables — a single-apartment walkthrough is very different from a developer launch. I quote one all-in number per shoot with no hidden extras, and you get the cinematic film plus social reels from one visit. Send the property and the deadline for a fixed quote.

Yes — from one visit you get the hero film, a smooth gimbal-led walkthrough for the portal and overseas buyers, and vertical reels cut for Instagram, TikTok and Shorts, all in one consistent look.
