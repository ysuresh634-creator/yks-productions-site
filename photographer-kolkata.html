Kolkata is the most naturally cinematic city in India — the trams, the yellow taxis, the north-Kolkata rajbaris, the light through old louvres. You don't decorate frames here; you get out of the city's way and light it honestly.

That's how I shoot it. Bengali weddings with their own rhythm and red-and-white palette, heritage and culture campaigns, and brand work that borrows the city's soul. Photo and video, one eye, one booking.

Shooting across Kolkata

North to Salt Lake, and the venues between.

Park Street and the Maidan, north Kolkata's rajbaris and Kumartuli, Ballygunge and Alipore, Salt Lake and New Town, the Hooghly ghats at dawn — and out to heritage venues in Chandannagar and beyond. Puja-season slots book early; plan ahead.

04

Common questions

Do you shoot Bengali weddings?

Yes — and the rituals set the shot list: gaye holud, topor, subho drishti, sindoor daan. I shoot them candid and cinematic, and deliver stills plus a film that feels like the day, not a highlight template.

Can you shoot during Durga Puja?

Yes — for brands and publications it's the city's biggest canvas. Pandal campaigns, street coverage and culture films; those dates disappear fast, so book well ahead.

Do you travel to Kolkata for a single event?

Yes. I'm based between Bangalore and Dubai and fly in for wedding days, campaigns and corporate shoots — travel is built into the one all-in quote.
