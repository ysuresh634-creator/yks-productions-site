Why NCR teams call me

Independent corporate film-making, without the chain

NCR is where many Indian head offices sit: corporate towers in Gurugram’s Cyber City and Golf Course Road, tech and media in Noida, and the policy and consulting world in central Delhi. That means a lot of corporate film work: profile films, leadership messages, CSR stories, culture and hiring videos, conference recaps.

The usual problem with corporate film is that it looks like corporate film: flat light, stiff interviews, a stock track. I come from cinematography, so I light interviews properly, record clean sound, find real b-roll in your office or plant, and cut to a pulse. You approve a treatment first, so there are no surprises in the edit.

Delhi is also one of India’s biggest fashion and ethnic-wear markets, and I shoot lookbooks and campaign films for labels as well as corporate work.

Locations & seasons

Shooting in Delhi, Gurugram and Noida

Delhi has some of the best architecture in the country for a brand film: Lutyens’ avenues, Lodhi Garden, Mehrauli, Hauz Khas and the old lanes of Chandni Chowk. Many monuments and protected sites need a filming permission, so I check that while planning rather than on the day. For corporate work, Gurugram and Noida campuses give clean, modern backdrops; building security and facilities passes go into the pre-production plan.

Seasons matter more in NCR than most cities. October to March gives the best outdoor light, while peak summer afternoons and winter smog both shape when we schedule exteriors. When a date can’t move, I design the film around interiors.

Who you’re hiring

One person, start to finish

I want to be straight about this, because most production-house websites aren’t. YKS Productions is me, Yedukrishna Suresh. There is no office, no account manager and no junior who turns up on the day instead of the person you briefed. I write the treatment, direct, shoot and edit.

When a brief genuinely needs more hands (a gaffer for a big lighting set-up, a sound recordist for interviews, a stylist or a make-up artist), I bring in freelancers for that project only. They report to me, they’re in the quote from the start, and you still deal with one person from first message to final file. If the shoot needs faces, I can cast them through YKS Talents.

What that means for a brand in Delhi NCR: one conversation instead of a chain of hand-offs, decisions made on set by the person who will edit the footage, and a quote that is one all-in number rather than a rate card with extras.

How it works

From brief to final cut

Brief

I come back with an approach (look, references, shot list outline) and one all-in number covering shoot, edit and any freelancers the brief needs.

Locations, permissions, casting, props and a call sheet. You approve the plan before anyone is booked, so the shoot day has no surprises.

FAQ

Questions brands ask first

Do you cover Gurugram and Noida as well as Delhi?

Yes, all of NCR. Many corporate shoots are in Gurugram and Noida; brand and fashion work often uses Delhi locations.

Are you an independent corporate film maker?

Yes. YKS Productions is independent and run by one person: I direct, shoot and edit, and bring in freelancers per project only when the brief needs them.

Can you film a conference or internal event?

Yes. Event recaps and highlight films are a common NCR brief. I plan the key moments with you beforehand so the recap tells a story rather than just showing a crowd.

Where are you based?

Bangalore. I travel for Delhi NCR shoots, and travel is included in the all-in quote.

How much does it cost?

Every project is quoted as one all-in number once I know the brief: number of shoot days, locations, cast, and how many cuts you need. There is no public rate card because two “brand films” can be completely different jobs. The fastest route is the 60-second quote form or a WhatsApp message.

Do you own the footage, or do we?

You get full usage rights to the delivered films and photos for your brand, including paid ads, as set out in the quote. If a cast member’s usage needs to be limited (for example to a period or a market), that is agreed up front.

Can you edit footage we already shot?

No. I edit what I shoot, because the edit is planned on set. I’m not an editor-for-hire for other people’s footage.
