Common questions

Are you from Kerala?

Yes — Malayali, with family roots here and Malayalam feature-film credits behind the camera. For a Kerala wedding or brand shoot that matters: the language, the rituals and the light are all native to me.

Do you shoot traditional Kerala weddings?

All of them — temple kalyanams, church nuptials and nikkahs, each with its own rhythm. Kasavu and gold photograph beautifully when lit honestly; I shoot candid, and deliver stills plus a cinematic film.

Do you work on film sets?

Yes — I've worked on Malayalam feature films, and I shoot on-set stills that respect the DoP's light and the set's discipline. Posters, stills and BTS in one visit.

Do you cover the rest of Kerala beyond Kochi?

Yes — Trivandrum to Kasaragod. Backwater resorts, Munnar estates, Thrissur weddings; travel inside Kerala is minor and always inside the all-in quote.
