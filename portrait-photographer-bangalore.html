A portrait isn't a documentation of a face — it's a decision about how someone should be seen. Most portrait and headshot work in Bengaluru flattens that down to a clean, well-lit, forgettable image. I come at it from a film background — cinematography and on-set stills for the South Indian film industry — so I light and direct a portrait the way a cinematographer frames a character: with intention, mood and presence.

That's what gets a callback for an actor, a second look for a founder's profile, and a frame a person actually wants to use. Whether it's a portfolio, a personal-brand set or a single signature headshot, the goal is the same: a portrait that feels like you on your best, most cinematic day.
