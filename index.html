13 — The eye behind the lens

Every frame should feel like a moment you'd want to relive.

I'm Yedukrishna Suresh. I grew up in Alappuzha — the backwater town on the Kerala coast where the light moves slow across the water and an ordinary afternoon already looks like a film. Kerala hands you cinema early, and Malayalam movies were the world I grew up inside long before I ever thought of standing behind a camera myself.

That pull carried me onto film sets — my craft comes from cinema. I've shot cinematography and on-set stills for the Malayalam features Soothravakyam and Baby Girl; on a set, the still is the film's first trailer, and the job is catching the single frame that makes you feel the whole story.

These days I move between Bangalore and Dubai — shooting fashion editorials, weddings, brand films and content for people who care how they're remembered. A designer lookbook in Bengaluru, a commercial film in Dubai: different cities, the same belief that a frame is worth slowing down for.

And I'm building beyond the camera. YKS now has a full marketing wing — an AI-powered operation where I run strategy, content, Google & Meta ads and lead generation for brands across India and the UAE, end to end. I'm also building Aureo Space, a creative growth studio. The camera taught me how attention works; now I build the whole machine around it.

“Photography and filmmaking were never two separate things for me — they're two ways of slowing a moment down until it means something.”

— Yedukrishna

Before you book

Questions I get asked most

How much does a professional photographer cost in India?

Most professional shoots in India are priced per day or per project, not per photo. Rates swing widely with the category — a product shoot, a fashion campaign and a wedding film are three different jobs with three different crews.

I quote one all-in number per project, covering the shoot, the edit and the final delivery, so there's nothing to add later. Tell me the brief and the dates and you'll get a straight figure.

Do you travel across India for shoots?

Yes — I shoot right across India, and in the UAE. Mumbai, Bangalore and Dubai are my regular bases, and I regularly travel to Delhi NCR, Hyderabad, Chennai, Pune, Kolkata, Ahmedabad, Jaipur, Kochi and Goa, plus Abu Dhabi and Sharjah.

Travel and stay are built into the project quote — you'll never get a surprise line item after the shoot.

Do you shoot both photo and video?

Both, usually on the same day. I'm a cinematographer and a stills photographer, so one booking can produce the campaign film, the social reels and the still images together.

That's normally cheaper and more consistent than hiring a separate photographer and videographer, because the lighting, styling and look are set up once.

What's included in a brand or product shoot?

Concept, casting, locations, the shoot, the edit, the colour grade and final delivery. If the shoot needs faces, I can source models and creators too.

You get files ready to publish — reels cut for vertical, stills retouched, and formats sized for the platforms you actually run.

How far in advance should I book a shoot?

Two to three weeks is comfortable for a brand or product shoot — enough time to lock concept, casting and location. Weddings and large campaigns are usually booked months ahead.

Short notice is often still possible depending on the calendar. Message me with the date and I'll tell you honestly whether it's doable.

Do you also run the marketing and ads, not just the shoot?

Yes — content is one half of what I do. I also run social media day to day, manage Google and Meta ad campaigns, generate leads and report on what every rupee returned.

You can see the whole scope at marketing.yksproductions.com.
