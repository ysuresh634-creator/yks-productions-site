Find talent in Dubai for a shoot

What brands cast for here: Luxury retail, hospitality, real estate, beauty and fine jewellery, for the UAE and wider Middle East.

What to put in a Dubai brief:

  • The product, the look you want and two or three reference images.
  • Dates, the location in or around Dubai, and how many looks.
  • Stills, video or both — and any spoken lines or language versions.
  • Usage: how long, where and on which channels the images will run, and any exclusivity.
  • A budget range, even a rough one.
