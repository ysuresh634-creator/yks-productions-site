It is free. There is no joining fee, no registration fee, no portfolio fee and no paid test shoot. If anyone asks you for money in the name of YKS Talents, it is not us.

Who can join YKS Talents in Dubai?

Women aged 18 to 34 who live in or regularly work in Dubai — Indian, South Asian and international talent alike — with a polished presence suited to luxury retail, hospitality, beauty and lifestyle work. Every application is read personally.

Poise and range: a couple of editorial looks, a lifestyle look that would sit naturally in a hotel or retail story, current natural-light digitals and accurate measurements. The free builder turns these into a comp card and digitals page you can use anywhere.

Yes. I cast my own YKS Productions shoots in Dubai from YKS Talents. Being on the platform makes you visible for those castings and to clients; it is not a promise of selection.
