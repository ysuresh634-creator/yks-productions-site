No. There is no joining fee, no registration fee, no portfolio fee and no paid test shoot. The portfolio builder is free and the PDF is yours to keep. If anyone asks you for money in the name of YKS Talents, it is not us.

A clean set of natural-light digitals, a few polished looks with a consistent grade, and accurate measurements. Because many Bangalore brands also shoot short video, a calm, confident piece to camera is worth adding when you have one.
