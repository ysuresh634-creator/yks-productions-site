A wedding isn't a list of shots to tick off. It's a day with a beginning, a middle and an end — and it deserves to be told like one. I cover weddings in Bangalore the way I'd shoot a film: watching for the real moments, lighting them so they last, and cutting it all into something you'll actually rewatch years from now, not file away once.

My background is in cinematography and stills for South Indian cinema, and I bring that same eye to a wedding — candid, unposed, and cinematic. Whether it's a traditional South Indian ceremony, a church wedding, a cross-cultural celebration or an intimate registration, the approach is the same: stay close to the emotion, stay out of the way, and come back with frames and a film that feel like the day actually felt.

Wedding questions

What do wedding packages in Bangalore cost?

Coverage is quoted per event based on days, functions and whether you want photography, film, or both. Tell me your dates and venues and I'll send a clear all-in package — no hidden costs.

Do you offer both photography and wedding films?

Yes. My team shoots candid photography and cinematic films together, so your stills and your film feel like one story, not two different styles.

How many photographers will cover my wedding?

Team size is matched to your event — usually two to four photographers and cinematographers for a full Indian wedding, so no moment on either side is missed.

Do you travel for destination weddings?

Yes. Based between Bangalore and Dubai, I regularly travel across India and abroad. Travel and stay are built transparently into the quote.

When do we get our photos and film?

A teaser of edited highlights within a week, the full gallery in 4–6 weeks, then your cinematic film and album.
