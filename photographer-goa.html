Shooting across Goa

North, South and the quiet parts between.

Anjuna, Vagator and Morjim in the north, Assagao's villas, Panjim and Fontainhas, Old Goa's chapels, down to Palolem, Agonda and the South's resort belt — plus river and forest locations inland. Sunset is scheduled first; everything else fits around it.

Common questions

Do you shoot destination weddings in Goa?

Yes — they're a staple. Multi-day coverage across venues, a team sized to the guest list, and travel plus stay built into one all-in quote so there are no surprise costs.

Can you plan a shoot around Goa's light?

That's the whole craft here. I schedule ceremonies and campaigns around golden hour and tide, and I know which beaches and villas shoot best at which time of day.

Do you shoot for resorts and villas?

Yes — stills, films and social reels made to convert lookers into bookings. One visit can produce a season's content bank.

How far ahead should I book a Goa date?

Season weddings (Nov–Feb) go early — three to six months out is safe. Campaigns and resort shoots usually need two to three weeks. Message the date and I'll confirm.
