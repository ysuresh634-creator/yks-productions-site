The person behind the lens

I'm Yedukrishna Suresh. I grew up in Alappuzha, on the Kerala coast, where an ordinary afternoon already looks like a film. My craft comes from cinema — cinematography and on-set stills in the South Indian film industry — most recently the Malayalam features Soothravakyam and Baby Girl.

These days I move between Bangalore and Dubai, shooting fashion editorials, weddings, real-estate films and brand content for people who care how they're remembered. Photography and filmmaking were never two separate things for me — they're two ways of slowing a moment down until it means something.
