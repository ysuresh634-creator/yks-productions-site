01What these terms are

These Terms of Engagement ("Terms") together with the quotation they are attached to form the whole agreement between YKS Productions ("YKS", "I") and the client named in the quotation ("Client", "you"). This is a contract under the Indian Contract Act, 1872.

02How the booking is confirmed

The quotation is an offer. A binding contract is formed on the earlier of:

  • (a) you confirming acceptance in writing (email, WhatsApp, or a signed copy of this document); or
  • (b) you paying the booking advance.
Paying the booking advance is itself acceptance of these Terms in full, whether or not anything is signed — acceptance by conduct under Section 8, Indian Contract Act, 1872. Acceptance by email or WhatsApp is valid under Section 10A, Information Technology Act, 2000, and electronic records are admissible under Section 63, Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023.

Until the advance is received, no date is held.

03Payment

StageWhen it is due
Booking advance — 50%On confirmation. The date is not held until this is received.
Balance — 50%Before delivery of the finished work, and in any event within 7 days of the shoot completing.
  • Amounts are in the currency stated on the quotation and are all-inclusive of fee, editing and normal post-production.
  • Payment counts only when funds are actually credited. Bank, remittance and conversion charges are yours.
  • Where you must deduct tax at source under the Income-tax Act, 1961, provide Form 16A within the statutory timeline.
  • All amounts are payable without set-off, counterclaim or deduction. A dispute about part of an invoice does not entitle you to withhold the remainder.

04Delivery is conditional on payment

The finished work is delivered only after the balance is received in full. Watermarked or low-resolution previews may be shared beforehand for selection and approval only. They are not a delivery, carry no licence, and publishing them before full payment is a breach of these Terms and an infringement of copyright.

Where files are released before payment to meet an agreed campaign date, the licence in clause 07 is granted conditionally on payment by the due date. If payment is not received within 15 days of that date, the licence terminates automatically and all use must cease.

05Approval, and what silence means

Selections, proofs or previews shared for your approval are deemed approved if you do not respond within 7 days of them being sent. Deemed approval has the same effect as written approval: the work is treated as complete and the balance falls due.

Where the balance is unpaid and you have not responded for 14 days after the gallery is made available, the engagement is treated as complete for invoicing purposes. The balance remains payable in full whether or not you download the files.

06Delivery media and drives

  • Digital delivery is the standard method and is included. Files are supplied by a secure link, active for 21 days.
  • Physical delivery is not included. Either you supply a new, unused drive of adequate capacity, or I purchase one billed to you at cost plus handling, quoted in advance.
  • A drive is a transfer medium, not a backup. All media fails. Once a drive leaves my hands I give no warranty on it and am not responsible for data loss, corruption or damage. Copy the contents to your own storage on receipt and verify them.
  • Where you supply the drive, I format it before writing. Anything already on it will be erased.
  • Courier charges are yours, and risk passes when the drive is handed to the courier.

07Storage, archival and backup

  • Delivered files are retained for 90 days from delivery as a courtesy — a grace period, not an archival service.
  • After 90 days files are deleted. Retaining material longer than needed is also what the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 expects where identifiable people appear in it.
  • Take your own backup on the day you receive the files. Two copies, two locations.
  • Re-supply within 90 days is charged as a retrieval fee. After 90 days there is no guarantee — if the files no longer exist they cannot be recovered at any price.
  • Long-term archival is available as a paid add-on agreed in writing before delivery.
  • Raw and unedited files are not delivered, licensed or sold. Editing is inseparable from the work.

08Copyright

Copyright in all photographs and films created under this engagement remains with YKS Productions. This clause is deliberate and is agreed between us.

Under Section 17(b), Copyright Act 1957, where a photograph is taken at the instance of any person for valuable consideration, that person is the first owner of the copyright — "in the absence of any agreement to the contrary." This clause is that agreement to the contrary.

On payment in full you receive a licence, not ownership:

  • Scope: use of the delivered work for the purposes described in the quotation.
  • Term and territory: perpetual and worldwide, unless the quotation says otherwise.
  • Non-exclusive, non-transferable and non-sub-licensable.
  • Personal to you. The licence does not extend to your vendors, venue, contractors, sponsors, partner brands, group companies or your own clients. Any third party wishing to use the work licenses it separately. If you supply the work to a third party who uses it, you are responsible for that use and the licence fee that would have applied.
  • Moral rights retained under Section 57, Copyright Act 1957. You may not distort or modify the work in a way prejudicial to my reputation.

I may show the work in my portfolio, website and social media unless an embargo is agreed in writing before the shoot.

09What you may not do with the work

  • Remove or alter the watermark, credit or embedded metadata. Rights management information is protected by Section 65B, Copyright Act 1957 — knowingly removing it is an offence carrying up to two years' imprisonment.
  • Circumvent any technical protection on previews or galleries — Section 65A.
  • Use the work to train or develop any artificial intelligence or machine-learning model, or submit it to a third-party service that does so. No such right is granted.
  • Re-sell, re-license or supply the work to another brand, agency or stock library.
  • Claim authorship of the work.

10People in the images, and personal data

  • Images of identifiable individuals are personal data under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. I handle them as a Data Fiduciary — collected for the agreed purpose, kept secure, erased when the purpose is served.
  • Where you arrange the people appearing in the work — staff, models, customers, guests or property — you confirm you have their informed consent for the photography and the intended commercial use. If you have not, tell me before the shoot.
  • Where I engage talent, the release is my responsibility.
  • Minors: no commercial use without verifiable parental or guardian consent.

11Late payment

  • Amounts unpaid after the due date carry interest at 1.5% per month (18% per annum) from the due date until actual payment, as agreed liquidated compensation under Sections 73 and 74, Indian Contract Act 1872.
  • Where YKS Productions is registered as a micro or small enterprise under the MSMED Act, 2006, the statutory rate under Section 16 applies where higher, with the timeline in Section 15.
  • Delivery, licence and further work stay suspended while any amount is overdue.
  • Reasonable recovery costs, including legal and court fees, are payable by you.

12Scope, revisions and additional work

  • The brief is what is written. Deliverables, locations, hours and formats in the quotation are the entire scope. Anything outside it is additional work, quoted separately.
  • Time on location: the quotation states the hours covered. Time beyond that is billed at the stated hourly rate. Delay caused by you, your venue or your guests counts as part of the booked hours — it does not extend them.
  • Revisions: the quotation includes a set number of rounds. A round is one consolidated set of feedback. Piecemeal feedback after a round has closed is chargeable.

13Standard of the work

The work is delivered to the professional standard reasonably expected of a photographer or cinematographer of my experience, consistent with the portfolio you saw before booking. My creative interpretation is part of what you are engaging, exercised within the agreed brief.

Payment is not conditional on subjective satisfaction. A genuine defect — technical failure, or a clear departure from the written brief — is corrected or reshot at my cost. Dissatisfaction with a creative choice that falls within the brief is not a defect and is not a ground to withhold payment.

14Cancellation and postponement

  • By you, more than 7 days before the shoot: the advance is retained as compensation for the date held and work already done.
  • By you, within 7 days: the full contract value is payable. A date cannot realistically be resold at that notice.
  • Postponement: one free postponement if requested more than 7 days before, subject to availability. A postponed shoot must be completed within 90 days of the original date, failing which it is treated as cancelled by you.
  • By me: I will either arrange a replacement of equivalent standard with your approval, or refund everything received. My liability is limited to that refund.

15On location

  • Access, permissions, location fees, parking and entry passes are arranged and paid by you.
  • I may decline to shoot, or stop shooting, where conditions are unsafe or unlawful, or required permissions have not been obtained. Where the cause is on your side, the engagement is treated as cancelled by you.
  • I may end the engagement immediately, with the full fee payable, where I or anyone working with me is subjected to abuse, harassment or an unsafe environment.
  • You are responsible for loss of or damage to my equipment caused by you, your staff, guests or contractors, at replacement cost.
  • For shoots exceeding six hours, please arrange drinking water, a meal, safe equipment storage and parking.

16Force majeure

Neither of us is liable for failure caused by events beyond reasonable control — natural disaster, flood, epidemic, government order, curfew, riot, war, or general failure of transport or power, reflecting Section 56, Indian Contract Act 1872. We reschedule in good faith and money already paid applies to the new date. For outdoor shoots made unsafe or unachievable by weather, we reschedule; non-refundable travel already booked is payable by you.

17Liability, confidentiality and assignment

  • Liability cap: my total liability, whatever the cause, is limited to the total amount you have actually paid me. I am not liable for indirect or consequential loss including loss of profit, business or opportunity. Nothing limits liability that cannot be limited by law.
  • Indemnity: you indemnify me against claims arising from permissions or consents you were responsible for, brand material you supplied, use outside the licence, and third-party claims relating to people or property you arranged.
  • Confidentiality: where you tell me in advance that a shoot is confidential, I will keep it so until you release it. Say so before the shoot — an embargo cannot be applied retrospectively.
  • No assignment: you may not transfer this agreement or the licence to another entity, including a group or successor company, without my written consent.

18General

  • Time of the essence applies to your payment obligations. Delivery timelines are estimates given in good faith.
  • No waiver: accepting a late payment or allowing an extra revision once does not waive the term.
  • Severability: if any clause is unenforceable, the rest continues and that clause is read down to the extent needed.
  • Survival: clauses 07, 08, 09, 10, 11 and 17 survive completion or termination.
  • Notices are validly given by email to the addresses below.
  • The written record: these Terms, the quotation and the written correspondence between us — including email and WhatsApp — form the whole agreement. Nothing said verbally varies them unless confirmed in writing by both of us.
  • Independent contractor: nothing here creates employment, partnership or agency.

Governed by the laws of India. The courts at Bangalore, Karnataka have exclusive jurisdiction, as permitted by Section 20, Code of Civil Procedure 1908. Before filing, we will attempt resolution in good faith for 15 days. Neither of us is prevented from seeking urgent interim relief.
