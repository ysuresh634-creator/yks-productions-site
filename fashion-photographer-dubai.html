Fashion Photographer in Dubai

A lookbook doesn’t sell clothes. It sells who someone becomes wearing them. Editorial, lookbooks, campaigns and model portfolios — shot like a film, not a catalogue. I'm Yedukrishna Suresh, founder of YKS Productions, shooting cinematic fashion across Dubai, building images that move the way light does on a set.

Book a Dubai shoot See fashion work

Most fashion photography in Dubai is lit to be flat and clean — safe for a catalogue, forgettable on a feed. I come at it from the other direction. My background is in cinematography and on-set stills for film, so I light a fashion shoot the way a cinematographer lights a scene: with intention, shadow and mood. The clothes still read perfectly. They just feel like they belong to a story.

That approach suits the brands and talent who want to stand out in a crowded market — designers launching a collection, models building a portfolio that gets callbacks, and founders who want their brand to look like it costs more than it does. If you've scrolled past a hundred identical Dubai fashion feeds and want frames that stop the thumb, that's the work I do.

03

Shooting across Dubai

The city is one of the best fashion backdrops in the world — I use all of it.

From clean-lined studio setups for campaign work, to the glass geometry of DIFC and Downtown, the raw industrial walls of Alserkal Avenue, the yachts and promenade of Dubai Marina, and the gold light of the desert at dawn — Dubai gives a fashion shoot more range than almost any city on earth. I scout the right backdrop for your brief, handle the permits where they're needed, and arrive knowing exactly how each location will read on camera at each hour of the day.

Fashion shoot questions

How much does a fashion shoot in Dubai cost?

Shoots are quoted per project, with half-day and full-day options depending on the number of looks, locations and deliverables. Send me the brief and I'll come back with an all-in rate — no hidden extras.

Do you arrange hair, makeup and styling?

Yes. I work with a trusted network of HMUAs and stylists in Dubai and can build the full creative team into your package, or work alongside a team you already have.

Do you shoot in studio or on location?

Both. Studio for controlled, clean campaign frames; location across the Marina, DIFC, Alserkal, Downtown and the desert when the brief wants atmosphere.

How soon do I get my images?

A first edit of selects within 3–5 days, fully retouched finals within 10–14. Rush turnaround is available for campaigns on a deadline.

Do you travel outside Dubai?

Yes — I'm based between Dubai and Bangalore and travel across the UAE and India for campaigns, designer collaborations and editorials.
