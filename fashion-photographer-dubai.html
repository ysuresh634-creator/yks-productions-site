03

Which shoot do you actually need

Brands often ask for one and describe another. The three are not interchangeable, and the difference shows up on the day.

What each kind of fashion shoot is for, and what it demands of the day.
KindWhat it is forHow the day runsWhat you end up with
Editorial Telling a story — a magazine feature, a season’s mood, a collaboration you want written about Fewer setups, more time in each. Location and light matter more than shot count A sequence that reads in order, with a strong opener and a closer
Lookbook Showing the garments clearly so a buyer or a customer can judge cut, drape and colour Fast and repetitive by design. Consistent light, consistent framing, straight through the rail Every piece shot cleanly, front and back, all matching each other
Campaign One or two hero images that will carry a season across every channel you own Slowest of the three. Most of the day goes into a handful of frames A small number of images, retouched hard, cropped for many placements
E-commerce Product pages, where the job is accuracy rather than atmosphere Locked-off setup, high volume, colour handled carefully so returns don’t rise A consistent set on clean background, sized for your storefront

Most briefs are really two of these at once — a lookbook that also has to yield a campaign image, say. That is entirely doable, but it has to be planned as two things sharing a day rather than discovered at four in the afternoon. Saying which one matters most is the single most useful sentence you can put in your first message.

Fashion shoot questions

What is the difference between an editorial, a lookbook and a campaign?

An editorial tells a story and is made to be published; a lookbook shows the garments clearly so a buyer can see cut, drape and colour; a campaign is built around one or two hero images that will carry a season across every channel. They need different shot counts, different pacing on the day and different edits, so the first thing worth settling is which of the three you actually need.

How much does a fashion shoot in Dubai cost?

Fashion shoots in Dubai are quoted per project, with half-day and full-day options depending on the number of looks, locations and deliverables. Tell me the brief and I will send an all-in rate with no hidden extras.

Do you arrange hair, makeup and styling?

Yes. I work with a trusted network of hair and makeup artists and stylists in Dubai, and can build the full creative team into your shoot package, or work alongside a team you already have.

Can you cast models for the shoot?

Yes. Casting can be handled as part of the job rather than as a separate hunt you run yourself, and the cost of the talent is folded into the one quoted number for the shoot. If you already have a face in mind or a model booked through an agency, I will work with them instead.

Do you shoot in studio or on location in Dubai?

Both. I shoot in Dubai studios for controlled lighting and clean campaign frames, and on location across the Marina, DIFC, Alserkal Avenue, Downtown and the desert when the brief calls for atmosphere.

How many looks can we shoot in a day?

A full day comfortably covers six to ten looks with one model, depending on how much the hair and makeup has to change between them. Changing the location as well as the look is what eats the day, so if the shot count matters more than variety, it is usually better to hold one location and move through the rail.

How soon do I receive the final images?

A first edit of selects is usually ready within 3 to 5 days, with fully retouched final images delivered within 10 to 14 days. Rush turnaround is available for campaigns on a deadline.

Who owns the images, and where can I use them?

You get the usage agreed in the quote, written down before the shoot rather than left vague. For most brands that means full use across your own channels, your website and paid social. If the images are going on out-of-home or into a market outside the UAE, say so at the brief stage, because it changes what the models are booked for.

Do you travel outside Dubai for fashion shoots?

Yes — across the UAE and to India regularly, since I work between Dubai and Bangalore. Travel is quoted inside the same all-in number rather than added afterwards.
