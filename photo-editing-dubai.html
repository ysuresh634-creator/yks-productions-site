01

Why it matters

Over-retouched photos are easy to spot and hard to trust. Plastic skin, warped backgrounds and colours that don’t match the product all cost credibility. My approach is the opposite: remove what’s distracting, keep the texture and character, and make a set of images look like they belong together.

I’ve edited fashion editorials, portraits, hospitality and brand work for clients including Marriott and Vaseline (Unilever), so I know the difference between an image for a billboard, a product page and an Instagram grid, and edit to the right standard for each.

03

Editing for Dubai brands and photographers

Dubai light is bright and hard for much of the year, and that shows up in images as blown skies, harsh skin shadows and colour casts from glass and LED. Fixing those well is a big part of editing for this city.

I edit for retail and e-commerce brands, hotels and restaurants, real estate agents, clinics, talent and other photographers who need a reliable retoucher for overflow work across Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Sharjah. Files can be sent online, so edit-only work does not need a meeting.

06

Common questions

Do you retouch photos taken by other photographers?

Yes. Edit-only work is welcome, for brands, talent and photographers who need overflow help. Send a few samples first so we can agree the standard.

Will the retouching look natural?

That’s the point. I keep skin texture, real product colour and true room proportions. If you want a more stylised beauty or editorial finish, we agree it on a test image first.

Which file types can you work with?

RAW files from most cameras, as well as high-quality JPEG and TIFF. RAW gives the best result for colour and highlight recovery.

Can you match my existing brand look?

Yes. Send a few images that represent your look and I’ll match new edits to them, or build a consistent grade if you don’t have one yet.

How much does photo retouching cost in Dubai?

It depends on the number of images and the level of retouching: a light batch edit and detailed beauty retouching are very different jobs. I quote per project after seeing samples.
