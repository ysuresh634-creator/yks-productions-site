LinkedIn Marketing & Personal Branding

For B2B companies, consultants and founders, LinkedIn is where buyers form opinions before they ever take a call. I help you show up there well: a clear personal brand, professional portraits and video, a posting rhythm, and LinkedIn ads when they make sense.

Recent work

01

Personal branding on LinkedIn

People buy from people. A founder who posts thoughtfully every week builds more trust than a company page posting announcements. Personal branding starts with clarity: what you want to be known for, who you want to reach and what you can say that others can’t. From there we build a small set of content pillars drawn from your real experience.

I then handle the parts most founders struggle with: professional portraits that don’t look like stock, short talking-head videos that are lit and miked properly (DJI wireless mic, Sony A7 IV), carousel ideas and a cadence you can keep up. You stay the voice; I make it easier and better-looking.

02

LinkedIn ads for B2B

LinkedIn ads let you reach decision-makers by job title, function, seniority, company size and industry. They work best for offers with real deal value: B2B services, SaaS, education, recruitment and professional firms. I typically use lead gen forms, document ads and thought-leadership ads built from a founder’s posts, with tight audiences and conversion tracking. It pairs with the rest of your performance marketing.

03

Company pages that look credible

Banner, About section, featured content, team photos and a showcase of real work. Small details, but buyers check them.

04

Who it suits

Founders raising awareness or funding, consultants and agencies selling high-value services, leadership teams at growing companies, and professionals moving into a new market, for example relocating between India and the UAE.

05

A realistic posting system

Most founders stop posting because it feels like a second job. The fix is a system: a monthly 60-minute idea session, a recording session for video every few weeks, a simple calendar, and drafts you only need to review. Consistency beats intensity.

We track a few useful signals: profile views, connection requests from the right people, inbound messages and meetings. Follower count matters less than who those followers are.

06

Personal branding beyond LinkedIn

A personal brand also lives on your website’s About page, Instagram, podcast appearances and press. The same portraits, story and positioning carry across all of them, which is why the photo and video session is planned to cover more than one platform.

07

Working across the UAE and India

Many founders I meet operate between Dubai and Indian cities like Bangalore, Mumbai and Delhi. LinkedIn is the one platform where both audiences meet, which makes a clear personal brand especially valuable if you work across both markets.
