For brands & productions

Hiring models in Mumbai for a shoot

Mumbai shoots range from one-day e-commerce and social content in Andheri and Goregaon studios to multi-day campaign and ad film productions. Most experienced talent here are used to quick turnarounds, so the deciding factors are usually dates, usage and exclusivity with competing brands rather than looks alone. Book early for pre-festive weeks, when the same faces are requested by several productions at once.

What brands cast for here: National campaigns, ad films, beauty, fashion editorial, e-commerce and social content.

What to put in a Mumbai brief:

  • The product, the look you want and two or three reference images.
  • Dates, the location in or around Mumbai, and how many looks.
  • Stills, video or both — and any spoken lines or language versions.
  • Usage: how long, where and on which channels the images will run, and any exclusivity.
  • A budget range, even a rough one.

I come back with suitable profiles from YKS Talents and arrange availability, fee, usage and the release with the talent. Everything goes through me, and talent contact details are never shared. The full process is in my guide on how to cast models for a brand shoot.

Questions, answered straight

Does YKS Talents offer modelling jobs in Mumbai?

No. YKS Talents does not offer jobs, placements or guaranteed work to anyone. It is a platform where clients casting in Mumbai find the right talent through your portfolio. Enquiries come through YKS Talents, so your contact details stay private.

Are there any joining or registration fees in Mumbai?

None. No joining fee, no registration fee, no portfolio fee and no compulsory or paid test shoot — now or later. If anyone asks you for money in the name of YKS Talents, it is not us.

Who is YKS Talents in Mumbai for?

Women aged 18 to 34 who live in or regularly work in Mumbai, with a polished, editorial presence suited to luxury, fashion and beauty work. Experience helps but is not required; a considered portfolio matters more.

What makes a portfolio stand out to Mumbai clients?

Range and honesty. Show two or three distinct looks with one consistent grade, a current set of unretouched digitals and exact measurements. The free builder lays this out as a comp card and digitals page in the format castings expect.

Does YKS cast its own Mumbai shoots from the platform?

Yes. I cast my own YKS Productions shoots in Mumbai from YKS Talents. Being on the platform makes you visible for those castings and to clients, but it is not a promise of selection or work.

How do brands find talent in Mumbai through YKS Talents?

Send the brief — the look, the dates, the usage. You receive a shortlist of suitable profiles with their work and one all-in number. Talent contact details are never published or passed on.

How do I hire a model in Mumbai for a shoot?

Send a brief on the casting brief form with the look, the dates, the location in Mumbai, the number of looks and where the images will run. I come back with suitable profiles from YKS Talents, confirm availability and agree fee, usage and the model release in writing. All contact goes through me; talent contact details are never shared.

How much does it cost to hire a model in Mumbai?

There is no single rate. Fees are agreed per project and depend mostly on usage (how long, where and on which channels the images run), plus shoot length, number of looks, travel and any exclusivity. Browsing YKS Talents and sending a brief are free. More on what moves the cost.
