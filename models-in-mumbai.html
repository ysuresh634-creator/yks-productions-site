Does YKS Talents offer modelling jobs in Mumbai?

No. YKS Talents does not offer jobs, placements or guaranteed work to anyone. It is a platform where clients casting in Mumbai find the right talent through your portfolio. Enquiries come through YKS Talents, so your contact details stay private.

Are there any joining or registration fees in Mumbai?

None. No joining fee, no registration fee, no portfolio fee and no compulsory or paid test shoot — now or later. If anyone asks you for money in the name of YKS Talents, it is not us.

Who is YKS Talents in Mumbai for?

Women aged 18 to 34 who live in or regularly work in Mumbai, with a polished, editorial presence suited to luxury, fashion and beauty work. Experience helps but is not required; a considered portfolio matters more.

What makes a portfolio stand out to Mumbai clients?

Range and honesty. Show two or three distinct looks with one consistent grade, a current set of unretouched digitals and exact measurements. The free builder lays this out as a comp card and digitals page in the format castings expect.

Does YKS cast its own Mumbai shoots from the platform?

Yes. I cast my own YKS Productions shoots in Mumbai from YKS Talents. Being on the platform makes you visible for those castings and to clients, but it is not a promise of selection or work.

How do brands find talent in Mumbai through YKS Talents?

Send the brief — the look, the dates, the usage. You receive a shortlist of suitable profiles with their work and one all-in number. Talent contact details are never published or passed on.
