Personal branding Wider, with room around you — the frame has to survive being cropped many different ways Open and approachable. It is an invitation, not a statement Two to three years, or until the work changes

04

How to turn up ready

Almost everything that goes wrong in a portrait was decided before the session started.

Clothes

Bring three, not one

Solid mid-tones photograph best. Bring one you feel unmistakably yourself in, one a notch sharper, and one you are unsure about — the unsure one wins more often than you would think.

Sleep

The night before matters

Unglamorous and completely true. Tiredness shows around the eyes before it shows anywhere else, and it is the one thing retouching handles badly.

Time

Don’t come straight from a meeting

Arriving with fifteen minutes spare is worth more than any lens. The first frames are almost always the stiffest ones.

Reference

Send one picture you like

Not to copy — to tell me what you mean. People describe wanting “natural” and mean four different things by it.

05

If you hate being photographed

Most people say some version of this in the first message, usually apologetically, as though it were unusual. It is not unusual — it is the default. Being looked at closely by a stranger holding a device is a genuinely odd thing to ask of somebody, and the self-consciousness that follows is the normal response, not a personal failing.

It is also a solvable problem, and solving it is most of the actual work. What does not help is being told to relax, or to smile, which converts a nervous person into a nervous person performing. What does help is having something specific to do — where to put your weight, where to look, what to think about a half second before the frame — because attention given to a task is attention taken away from being watched.

That is a directing skill rather than a photographic one, and it is the part of a film background that transfers most directly. On a set the job is getting a real expression out of someone on a schedule, with a crew standing around. A portrait session is a gentler version of the same problem. It is why the sessions run longer than the shooting strictly requires: the first ten minutes are rarely the ones we use.

08

Portrait questions

Do you do personal branding portraits?

Yes — personal branding is the most common reason people book a portrait session in Dubai. The aim is a set you can use across a website, a speaker profile, press and social, shot wide enough that the same frame survives being cropped several different ways.

What is the difference between a portrait and a headshot?

A headshot answers one question — what do you look like — and is built for a small frame such as a profile photo. A portrait is allowed to say something about you as well, so it is framed wider, lit with more character and directed for a real expression rather than a neutral one. If you only need the profile photo, the headshot is the quicker and cheaper job.

Editorial portraits and model portfolios?

Both, regularly. Editorial portraits are composed for a page and can afford to be serious. Portfolio work is the opposite discipline — clean, honest and varied, because an agency needs to see range and the real face rather than the styling.

What does a portrait session cost?

It depends on the number of looks, whether we are in a studio or on location, whether you want hair and makeup, and how many final retouched images you need. I quote one all-in figure covering the session and the retouching, with nothing added afterwards.

Studio or location?

Both work and they say different things. A studio gives control and a clean, timeless result. Location in Dubai gives context and warmth, and suits personal branding where you want to look like you belong somewhere. If you are unsure we start with the safe frame and then try the more interesting one.

How long does a session take?

Most portrait sessions run between one and two hours. That is longer than the shooting strictly needs, deliberately — the first ten minutes are rarely the ones we use, and the extra time is what separates a photo you tolerate from one you actually like.

I am not photogenic. Can you still get something good?

Almost everyone says this, which is a fair sign it is about the experience rather than the face. Being photographed is uncomfortable by default. The fix is direction — being told where to put your weight and where to look, so your attention is on a task instead of on being watched — and that is most of what the session is.

How soon do I get my photos?

A gallery of edited selects usually lands within 3 to 5 days, with final retouched images following shortly after. Rush delivery is available if you need them for a launch or a deadline.

Do you photograph couples and families as well?

Yes. Couple portraits work in the same way as any other portrait — the direction just has to give two people something to do together rather than one person something to do alone. Maternity and family sessions are handled on their own page.
