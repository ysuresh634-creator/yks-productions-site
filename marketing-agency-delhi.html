01

One region, three very different markets

Delhi NCR is really three markets. Gurugram is corporate: MNC offices, consulting firms and fast-growing startups along Cyber City and Golf Course Road. Noida has media houses, IT services and a large education sector. Delhi itself has some of India’s strongest fashion designers, premium hospitality, and a huge wedding and events economy. Marketing that works in one doesn’t always work in the others.

I plan campaigns with that in mind, targeting by area and audience, and producing content that fits the category: polished corporate video for Gurugram, fashion-led visuals for South Delhi brands, lead-generation for education and services.

02

Common Delhi NCR engagements

Corporate and B2B: LinkedIn marketing, leadership personal branding and corporate video. Education and coaching: Google Search and Meta lead generation with landing pages and clear tracking. Fashion and design labels: lookbooks, reels and Meta ads. Hospitality, events and weddings: content that shows atmosphere and craft.

03

Lead generation done carefully

Lead-heavy sectors in NCR often suffer from low-quality leads. I set up qualifying questions on forms, conversion tracking that feeds back to the platforms, and simple reporting so you can see cost per real enquiry, not just cost per form fill.

04

Across NCR

Shoot days in Connaught Place, Hauz Khas, Saket, Mehrauli, Vasant Kunj, Gurugram Cyber City, Golf Course Road and Noida, planned around travel.

05
