A softer eye

Family photography isn't about perfect poses — it's about catching the real ones: the laugh, the cuddle, the way a newborn curls into a shoulder. A film background trains you to watch for those unrepeatable seconds and to use soft, natural light that flatters everyone. Add a calm, patient pace that works with kids instead of against them, and you get a gallery that feels less like a photoshoot and more like a memory.

Maternity & family questions

Where do you shoot sessions in Dubai?

On location — beach at golden hour, a park, the desert, or your own home — or in studio. Outdoor soft-light sessions are gorgeous; home sessions are perfect for newborns.

When should I book my maternity shoot?

Usually around 30–34 weeks for maternity, when the bump is round but you're still comfortable. For newborns, the first two weeks — so book ahead around your due date.

Are sessions relaxed with young kids?

Completely — playful and unhurried, following the kids rather than forcing poses. The best frames are the real, in-between moments.

What do I get, and how fast?

A warm, naturally-edited gallery within about a week, sized for print and sharing. Prints and albums can be arranged.

How much does it cost?

Depends on session length and location. One all-in price, no hidden extras. Tell me what you're celebrating and roughly when.
