Booking models & talent — the questions I get

How casting works through YKS, in India and the UAE.

How do I book a model in Dubai or India?

Send the brief — the look you're after, the city, the dates and what it's for. I come back with a shortlist from the roster and one all-in number covering the talent and the shoot. You book through me, not through the talent directly.

Do you work as a model agency?

No. I'm a photographer and director who casts and coordinates talent as part of production. You get one all-in number covering the talent and the shoot together, and you deal with one desk from brief to delivery.

What does booking a model cost?

It's quoted per project rather than per hour, because the number depends on usage — how long you need the images, where they run, and whether it's editorial, commercial or campaign. Tell me the brief and you get one figure covering talent and shoot together.

Can you cast for a shoot you're not photographing?

Yes. Casting and coordination can be booked on their own if you already have a photographer or a crew.

Which cities can you cast in?

Across India — Bangalore, Mumbai, Delhi, Kochi, Hyderabad, Chennai, Goa — and across the UAE, mainly Dubai and Abu Dhabi. Talent travels for the right project, with travel built into the quote.

Do you handle releases and usage rights?

Yes. Model releases and usage terms are agreed before the shoot and documented, so you know exactly where and how long you can run the images.

Can you find a specific look — expat, Indian, international?

Usually. Tell me what the campaign needs and I'll come back with options, or source beyond the current roster if nothing on it fits.

I'm a model — how do I join?

Use the apply link on this page. Send portfolio or Instagram, your city, and what you're available for. There's no fee to join the roster.
