The difference

Do I need a model agency for this?

For a campaign with a name face and a seven-figure media spend behind it, yes — go to an agency. For the shoot most brands actually need this quarter, an agency adds a booking commission, a second set of dates to align and a third party between you and the person holding the camera. Here's the honest comparison.

Through an agency

  • Agency commission on top of the talent fee, typically 20–25%
  • Talent booked separately from the crew — two contracts, two schedules
  • Casting desk doesn't see the pictures until you do
  • Usage negotiated per campaign, often after the shoot
  • Minimum booking sizes that don't suit a one-day content shoot

Through this desk

  • One all-in number covering the talent and the shoot together
  • One contract, one schedule, one person accountable for the day
  • The person casting the face is the person lighting it
  • Usage agreed in writing before the shoot, not after
  • Works at one face for a day, or a full campaign line-up

I'm not a model agency and don't pretend to be one. I'm a photographer and director who casts as part of production — which is why there's no commission line on your invoice. More on how casting runs in India →

Usage & rights

What am I actually buying when I book a model?

You're not buying the photographs. You're buying permission to use someone's face, for a defined purpose, in defined places, for a defined length of time. Get that wrong and you either overpay for rights you'll never use, or run a campaign you don't have permission for. It's the part of a quote worth reading twice.

Term

How long you can run the images. Six months and twelve months are the usual marks for social and digital; campaign work is often two years. Perpetual costs more because the talent can never re-license that look to anyone else.

Territory

Where they can run. India-only is the common default. Adding the Gulf, or going worldwide, changes the number — which is why the same face costs differently for a Bangalore D2C launch and a Dubai retail campaign.

Channels

Organic social is the cheapest. Paid social and performance ads cost more because the images work harder. Out-of-home, print and packaging cost the most, because the face becomes the brand for the duration.

Exclusivity

Whether the talent can shoot a competitor during your term. Most shoots don't need it. If yours does, it has to be bought — and it should be on the quote, not assumed.

All four go on the quote before the shoot, in plain English. If you're comparing quotes from elsewhere and one of them is cheaper, this is usually where the difference is hiding.
