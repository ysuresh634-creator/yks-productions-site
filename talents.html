The difference

Do I need a model agency for this?

For a campaign with a name face and a seven-figure media spend behind it, yes — go to an agency. For the shoot most brands actually need this quarter, an agency adds a booking commission, a second set of dates to align and a third party between you and the person holding the camera. Here's the honest comparison.

Through an agency

  • Agency commission on top of the talent fee, typically 20–25%
  • Talent booked separately from the crew — two contracts, two schedules
  • Casting desk doesn't see the pictures until you do
  • Usage negotiated per campaign, often after the shoot
  • Minimum booking sizes that don't suit a one-day content shoot

Through this desk

  • One all-in number covering the talent and the shoot together
  • One contract, one schedule, one person accountable for the day
  • The person casting the face is the person lighting it
  • Usage agreed in writing before the shoot, not after
  • Works at one face for a day, or a full campaign line-up

I'm not a model agency and don't pretend to be one. I'm a photographer and director who casts as part of production — which is why there's no commission line on your invoice. More on how casting runs in India →

Usage & rights

What am I actually buying when I book a model?

You're not buying the photographs. You're buying permission to use someone's face, for a defined purpose, in defined places, for a defined length of time. Get that wrong and you either overpay for rights you'll never use, or run a campaign you don't have permission for. It's the part of a quote worth reading twice.

Term

How long you can run the images. Six months and twelve months are the usual marks for social and digital; campaign work is often two years. Perpetual costs more because the talent can never re-license that look to anyone else.

Territory

Where they can run. India-only is the common default. Adding the Gulf, or going worldwide, changes the number — which is why the same face costs differently for a Bangalore D2C launch and a Dubai retail campaign.

Channels

Organic social is the cheapest. Paid social and performance ads cost more because the images work harder. Out-of-home, print and packaging cost the most, because the face becomes the brand for the duration.

Exclusivity

Whether the talent can shoot a competitor during your term. Most shoots don't need it. If yours does, it has to be bought — and it should be on the quote, not assumed.

All four go on the quote before the shoot, in plain English. If you're comparing quotes from elsewhere and one of them is cheaper, this is usually where the difference is hiding.

Booking models & talent — the questions I get

How casting works through YKS, in India and the UAE. If yours isn't here, ask it in the brief and I'll answer it straight.

How do I book a model in Dubai or India?

Send the brief — the look you're after, the city, the dates and what it's for. I come back inside 24 hours with a shortlist from the roster and one all-in number covering the talent and the shoot. You book through me, not through the talent directly.

What does booking a model cost in India?

It's quoted per project rather than per hour, because the number depends on usage — how long you need the images, where they run, and whether it's editorial, commercial or campaign. A one-day content shoot and a two-year national campaign can use the same face and cost very differently. Tell me the brief and you get one figure covering talent and shoot together.

Why is the roster not fully public?

Because publishing a page of photographs, measurements and cities is how those images end up scraped, reposted and used to catfish. Talent on the edit are put forward against a brief instead. You lose nothing — you still see the faces, the work and the range before you commit — and they keep control of where their pictures live.

Do you work as a model agency?

No. I'm a photographer and director who casts and coordinates talent as part of production. That means no agency commission on top of the talent fee, one contract instead of two, and the person who cast the face is the person lighting it on the day.

How fast can you get me a face?

Shortlist inside 24 hours of the brief. For a straightforward one-face content shoot in a city I cast in regularly, a confirmed booking in two to three working days is normal. Same-week shoots are possible; tell me the date up front and I'll say honestly whether it's realistic.

Can you cast for a shoot you're not photographing?

Yes. Casting and coordination can be booked on their own if you already have a photographer or a crew. You get the same shortlist, holds and paperwork, and I hand over a fully briefed talent on the day.

Which cities can you cast in?

Across India — Bangalore, Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Hyderabad, Chennai, Pune, Kolkata, Ahmedabad, Jaipur, Chandigarh, Kochi and Goa — and across the UAE, mainly Dubai and Abu Dhabi. Talent travel for the right project, with travel built into the quote rather than added afterwards.

Do you handle model releases and usage rights?

Yes, and before the shoot rather than after. The release and the usage terms — territory, term and channels — are agreed in writing before anyone arrives on set, so you know exactly where and how long you can run the images, and there's no renewal surprise later.

What if the model doesn't turn up, or doesn't work out?

Dates are held in writing once you confirm, and if someone drops out I recast and the shoot date stands. You approve the final face before anything is booked, so nobody is cast off a single photograph.

Can you find a specific look — expat, Indian, international?

Usually. Tell me what the campaign needs and I'll come back with options, or source beyond the current roster if nothing on it fits. I'd rather say "not on the edit, give me two days" than sell you the nearest available face.

Do you cast for regional-language and film work?

Yes — actors for branded films, TVCs and narrative work, across South Indian languages and Hindi. Tell me the language and the register in the brief; it changes the shortlist more than the look does.
