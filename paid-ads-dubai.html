01

Why it matters

Most underperforming ad accounts don’t have a targeting problem. They have a creative problem (ads that look like ads and get skipped) and a tracking problem (the platform optimises for clicks or form fills instead of real customers). Fix those two and the same budget usually goes further.

That is why I run ads and produce the creative together. I’m a film-trained photographer and videographer, so the ads are shot for the platform, with multiple hooks to test, and the campaign is set up to learn from real enquiries, calls and bookings. Everything runs in your own ad accounts, so you keep the data and the history.

03

Which platform for Dubai?

There is no single best platform. Google Search usually wins for urgent, high-intent services. Meta is strong for hospitality, beauty, real estate and lifestyle offers, especially with click-to-WhatsApp. TikTok and Snapchat reach younger audiences and reward native, creator-style content. LinkedIn suits B2B. I recommend a mix based on your offer, budget and where your customers already are, and say so when a platform isn’t worth it for you.

Dubai ad costs move with the season: competition rises in the cooler months, around Ramadan and Eid, and at year-end. Campaigns and creative are planned ahead so you are not paying peak prices with tired ads.

Common questions

Whose ad account do you use?

Yours. Accounts are opened or kept in your business’s name, with me added as a user, so you own the data and history and can stop at any time.

Which ad platform is best in Dubai?

It depends on what you sell. Google Search suits high-intent services, Meta suits most consumer, hospitality and real estate offers, TikTok and Snapchat reach younger audiences, and LinkedIn suits B2B. I recommend a mix after the audit.

Do you create the ads too?

Yes. I’m a photographer and videographer, so ad creative is shot and edited specifically for each platform, with several hooks to test. That is often where the biggest improvements come from.

What budget do I need to start?

It depends on the platform, competition and your goals. I’ll recommend a realistic test budget in the audit, and the ad spend is paid directly by you to the platform, separate from my fee.

How is ads management priced?

As a fixed monthly management fee agreed up front, with ad spend paid directly to the platforms by you. Creative production is quoted with it so there is one clear number.
