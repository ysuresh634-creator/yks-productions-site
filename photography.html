Recent work

01

Product photography

Product photography has two jobs: show exactly what someone is buying, and make them want it. I shoot hero images with controlled light and simple sets, detail shots for texture and finish, and lifestyle frames that show the product in use. The 85mm and 50mm primes on the Sony A7 IV keep products true to shape without distortion.

02

E-commerce photography

E-commerce photography is about consistency. Every product in a catalogue should share the same angles, lighting, background and framing so the store looks professional and customers can compare easily. I set up a repeatable lighting plan, shoot to marketplace requirements (such as clean white backgrounds for Amazon, Noon or Flipkart listings), and deliver images named and organised by SKU. Lifestyle and on-model shots can be added from the same session.

03

Food & restaurant photography

Food photography lives or dies by timing and light. I work with the kitchen to plate in batches, shoot hero dishes while they’re at their best, and capture the room, the team and the atmosphere so the restaurant’s story is told, not just the menu. The same shoot can produce reels for social. For Dubai see food photographer in Dubai.

04

Event photography & videography

Conferences, launches, parties, brand activations and private celebrations. Event work needs discretion and anticipation: being in the right place for the moment before it happens. I can cover both photo and video, delivering a quick set of highlights for same-week social posts and the full gallery afterwards. See Dubai and Bangalore.

05

Fashion, real estate & wedding photography

These have their own dedicated portfolios: fashion photography (editorials, lookbooks and campaigns), real estate photography & videography (listings, developments and hospitality) and wedding photography & videography (ceremonies, haldi, pre-wedding and couple portraits).

06

Lenses and light, briefly

I shoot on a Sony A7 IV with Sony 28mm, 50mm and 85mm primes. The 28mm is for spaces and context (restaurants, offices, events); the 50mm is the all-rounder; the 85mm is for portraits and products where shape and compression matter. Lighting is planned around the subject: soft and controlled for products, natural and moody for food and interiors, discreet for events.

07

Delivery and usage

Images are selected, colour-corrected and delivered in web and print sizes, organised for how you’ll use them: website, marketplace, social and ads. Usage rights for your own channels are standard; wider usage is agreed in the quote.

08

Photography as part of marketing

Photos rarely live alone. Product images feed ads and your store; food photos feed delivery apps and Instagram; event photos feed LinkedIn and press. When photography is planned alongside your marketing, every image has a job.

Common questions

Do you photograph products in a studio?

I don’t run a studio of my own. I shoot on location or in a rented studio when the brief needs one, and small products can be shot with a portable setup at your office or warehouse.

Can you shoot for Amazon, Noon or Flipkart listings?

Yes. I shoot to the marketplace’s image requirements and deliver files organised by SKU.

Do you cover events with both photo and video?

Yes, either one or both, depending on the event size and what you need afterwards.

Which cities?

Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Bangalore, Mumbai and Delhi NCR, plus other cities on request.
