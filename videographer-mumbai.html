Video for a city that knows good cinema

02

What I shoot in Mumbai

Fashion and beauty films: lookbook videos, campaign reels and behind-the-scenes. Brand films and commercials for D2C and consumer brands. Corporate video for BKC and Lower Parel offices: leadership messages, culture and recruitment films. Hospitality: hotels, restaurants and bars in Bandra, Juhu and Colaba. Real estate: walkthroughs and lifestyle films for new projects.

03

Shooting in Mumbai, practically

Mumbai shoots need planning around traffic, permissions and monsoon. I schedule locations by area to avoid wasted hours, plan indoor alternatives between June and September, and work as a one-person crew so I can fit into tight spaces like apartments, cafes and studios.

04

Brand films, reels and corporate in one trip

Mumbai clients often get the most value by combining needs: a brand film, a set of reels and leadership portraits from the same visit. I plan the day so each setup is used for more than one deliverable, and capture stills alongside video. Footage is shot and delivered in the formats agreed at the start: horizontal masters, vertical versions and selects. See also video production and content creation.

Mumbai questions

Do you travel to Mumbai for shoots?

Yes. Shoots are scheduled as dedicated trips, often batching several deliverables into one or two days.

What kit do you use?

Sony A7 IV with 28mm, 50mm and 85mm primes, a DJI RS3 gimbal and DJI wireless mic.

Do you do drone shots?

No. I don’t fly drones; a licensed operator can be added if needed.

Do you also take photos on the same shoot?

Yes. Stills and video can be captured on the same day.
