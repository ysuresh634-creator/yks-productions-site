Brand film vs ad film

What’s the difference, and which do you need?

Brand filmAd film (digital)
JobTells who you are and why you existGets a specific action: click, buy, sign up
LengthUsually 60–120 s6, 15 or 30 s, often in several versions
Where it runsHomepage, launch, LinkedIn, decks, eventsMeta, YouTube, OTT pre-roll
How it’s judgedDoes it make people feel something about the brand?Hook rate, watch time, cost per result
Best togetherOne shoot, planned for bothCut-downs from the brand film footage

Most brands need both, and the efficient way is one production planned for both: the brand film as the master, and ad cuts designed into the shot list from the start, not chopped out afterwards.

Approach

How I make ad films that work

Hook first. On Meta and YouTube, the first second decides whether anyone sees the rest. Every ad I make has its opening frame and line planned in the treatment, and I’ll often shoot two or three alternative openings so you can test them.

Made for the platform. Vertical isn’t a crop of horizontal. Where an ad will run 9:16, it’s framed for 9:16 on the day, with text-safe areas and captions planned.

Cinema craft, digital speed. Real lighting, motivated camera movement, clean sound and a proper grade, but without a broadcast-scale unit, so the budget goes on screen.

If your brief needs a broadcast TVC with a large unit, a celebrity and agency approvals at every stage, a large ad-film house is the better fit and I’ll tell you so.

FAQ

Questions brands ask first

What is a brand film?

A brand film tells the story of a company: what it believes, who it serves and why it exists, rather than pushing one product. It usually runs 60 to 120 seconds and is cut into shorter versions for ads and social.

How long should an ad film be?

For digital, plan for 6, 15 and 30-second versions. Six seconds works as a reminder, 15 as the workhorse on Meta and YouTube, and 30 when the product needs explaining. I plan all three into one shoot.

Do you write the concept, or do we?

Either. I can write the concept and treatment from your brief, or produce an idea your team or agency already owns.

Can you make an ad film with actors or models?

Yes. I cast through YKS Talents or work with talent you’ve booked. Usage terms are agreed in the quote.

How much does it cost?

Every project is quoted as one all-in number once I know the brief: number of shoot days, locations, cast, and how many cuts you need. There is no public rate card because two “brand films” can be completely different jobs. The fastest route is the 60-second quote form or a WhatsApp message.

Do you own the footage, or do we?

You get full usage rights to the delivered films and photos for your brand, including paid ads, as set out in the quote. If a cast member’s usage needs to be limited (for example to a period or a market), that is agreed up front.

Can you edit footage we already shot?

No. I edit what I shoot, because the edit is planned on set. I’m not an editor-for-hire for other people’s footage.
