No. No joining fee, no registration fee, no portfolio fee and no paid test shoot. The builder is free and the PDF is yours to keep. Anyone asking you for money in our name is not us.

Who is YKS Talents in Kolkata for?

Women aged 18 to 34 who live in or regularly work in Kolkata, with a polished presence suited to festive, bridal, handloom, jewellery and editorial work. Experience helps but is not required.

Ideally a few months before the festive season, when Puja and wedding campaigns are being cast. Include a traditional look in handloom or a heritage saree, a contemporary editorial look, and fresh natural-light digitals with exact measurements.

Yes. I cast my own YKS Productions shoots in Kolkata from YKS Talents, including festive and textile campaigns. Being on the platform makes you visible for those castings and to clients; it is not a promise of selection.
