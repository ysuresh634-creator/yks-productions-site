How Much Does Social Media Management Cost in Dubai?

Most Dubai brands pay between AED 3,000 and AED 15,000 a month — but the number that matters isn't the retainer, it's how much real, scroll-stopping content it actually buys. Here's the honest breakdown.

Updated June 2026 · by Yedukrishna Suresh

The typical ranges in 2026

LevelPer month (AED)Usually includes
Basic2,000 – 5,000Scheduling, captions, light graphics — often little or no original shooting
Content-led5,000 – 12,000A monthly shoot, reels + photos, strategy, posting and reporting
Full12,000 – 25,000+Heavy content volume, multi-platform, ads support, end-to-end management

Ranges, not fixed prices. The single biggest variable is how much original content — especially video — gets shot each month. That's also what actually drives results. My own social media service is content-led and quoted all-in per month.

Why the cheapest option rarely works

A AED 1,500/month "we'll post for you" package sounds great until you realise it's recycled stock and AI captions. Instagram and TikTok reward original, native, scroll-stopping content — overwhelmingly video now. If nobody's actually shooting for your brand, you're paying someone to keep a dead account warm. The money is better spent on fewer, better pieces of real content.

What actually drives the price

  • Content volume. How many reels and posts, and how often you shoot, is the main cost driver.
  • Shot, or just scheduled? A retainer that includes a monthly shoot costs more — and is worth far more.
  • Number of platforms. One channel done well beats five done badly; more channels means more cost.
  • Strategy & ads. Adding paid-ad management and reporting raises the retainer.
  • One operator vs an agency. A single creative who shoots and manages is usually leaner than an agency that outsources the camera.

The smart way to buy it

  • Ask "who actually shoots the content?" — if they outsource it, the quality will show.
  • Prioritise video — it's what the algorithm pushes.
  • Start focused — one or two platforms done brilliantly beats spreading thin.
  • Look for an all-in monthly number so there are no surprise charges.

The short version

Budget AED 5,000–12,000/month for genuinely content-led social media in Dubai — and judge it on the quality of what's actually shot, not the number of posts. See what content-led management looks like on my social media marketing page.
