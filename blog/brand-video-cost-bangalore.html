What Affects the Cost of a Brand Video in Bangalore

Two brand videos of the same length can differ enormously in effort.

Updated October 2026 · by Yedukrishna Suresh

Get a quote See the work

01

Why there’s no single price

Two brand videos of the same length can differ enormously in effort. A founder talking to camera in one office is a very different job from a multi-location product film with actors. Rather than quote a range that would mislead you, here are the factors that actually move the number, so you can brief well and compare quotes fairly.

02

1. Shoot days

The biggest driver. Bangalore traffic matters here: two locations in Whitefield and Koramangala can eat half a day in transit. Grouping locations by area and planning an efficient shot list often saves an entire shoot day.

03

2. Crew size

A compact crew (a videographer with a gimbal and wireless audio, like my Sony A7 IV and DJI RS3 setup) is right for most startup and corporate videos. Bigger productions add a director, gaffer, sound recordist, makeup and assistants, each adding cost and coordination.

04

3. Pre-production

Scripting, storyboards, location scouting, casting and permissions. More planning usually means less shoot time and fewer surprises, so it’s rarely wasted.

05

4. Talent and locations

Using your own team is cheapest. Professional actors, models or voice-over artists add fees, and sometimes usage fees. Studios, cafes and premium venues may charge for location time.

06

5. Versions and post-production

One horizontal master is different from a master plus vertical cut-downs, subtitled versions and stills. Decide where the video will live (website, YouTube, LinkedIn, Instagram ads) before the shoot so the right formats are captured on the day.

07

6. Usage

Organic use on your own channels is standard. Paid ad usage, broadcast or long-term usage of talent can change the cost.

08

Quick answers

Can I reduce the cost without hurting quality?

Yes: fewer locations, your own team on camera, a tight script and batching several videos into one shoot day.
