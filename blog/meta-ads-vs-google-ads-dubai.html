Meta Ads vs Google Ads for Dubai Businesses

Google Ads captures demand that already exists; Meta Ads (Facebook and Instagram) creates demand that doesn’t exist yet.

Updated October 2026 · by Yedukrishna Suresh

Get a quote See the work

01

The short answer

Google Ads captures demand that already exists; Meta Ads (Facebook and Instagram) creates demand that doesn’t exist yet. If people in Dubai already search for what you sell, such as “AC repair Al Barsha” or “corporate video production Dubai”, Google Search should usually come first. If your product sells on looks, lifestyle or emotion, like a new restaurant, a fashion label or an off-plan launch, Meta usually comes first. Most growing businesses end up using both.

02

How Google Ads works for Dubai businesses

On Google Search you bid on keywords and your ad appears when someone searches. The intent is high, which is why it suits services and urgent needs. Success depends on tight keyword selection, negative keywords to filter out irrelevant searches (job seekers, DIY, other emirates if you don’t serve them), ads that match the search closely, and a landing page that answers the question immediately. Location targeting matters in the UAE: decide whether you serve all of Dubai, specific areas or the wider UAE, and whether to include people merely interested in Dubai.

03

How Meta Ads works for Dubai businesses

On Instagram and Facebook your ad interrupts someone’s feed, so the creative does most of the work. Targeting is increasingly automated, meaning the platform finds buyers based on who responds to your ad. That makes creative variety the main lever: different hooks, formats (reels, stories, carousels, statics) and angles. Dubai’s audience is highly visual and international, so creative that tells the story with images and short on-screen text tends to travel across languages better than heavy copy.

04

Which categories lean which way

Lean Google: home services, clinics and treatments, legal and business setup, B2B services, car rental, urgent needs. Lean Meta: restaurants and cafes, fashion and beauty, events, real estate launches, D2C products, fitness. Both: real estate brokers, hotels, education, premium services where buyers both search and browse.

05

Tracking first, either way

Neither platform can optimise without good data. Before spending, set up conversion tracking: the Meta pixel and Conversions API, Google Ads conversions or GA4 imports via Tag Manager, and a clear definition of a real lead such as a WhatsApp click, a form submission or a call. Without this you are paying for clicks, not customers.

06

A sensible way to start

Start with the platform that matches your demand type, run it properly with tracking and enough creative or keyword coverage to learn, and add the second platform once the first is stable. Review on cost per real enquiry, not clicks or likes. If you want help choosing, see performance marketing or get in touch for a quote.

Quick answers

Is Google Ads or Meta Ads cheaper in Dubai?

Costs vary by category, season and competition, and change constantly, so the honest answer is: compare on cost per real enquiry in your own account, not on headline click prices.

Can I run both from the start?

Yes, if you have enough budget and creative to give each a fair test. Otherwise start with one.

Do I need Arabic ads?

It depends on your audience. Many Dubai campaigns run in English; Arabic versions help when your buyers prefer it.
