Marketing Agency vs Consultant Which Do You Need?

Agency: a team covering strategy, creative, media and account management.

Updated October 2026 · by Yedukrishna Suresh

Get a quote See the work

01

Three models

Agency: a team covering strategy, creative, media and account management. Consultant: an experienced individual who sets direction and often guides your in-house team. Freelancer: a specialist executing one thing, such as ads, design or video. Each fits a different stage.

02

When an agency makes sense

You need many channels running at once, large volumes of creative, and you have the budget for a team. The trade-off is layers: your brief passes through account managers, and the senior people who pitched may not do the work.

03

When a consultant makes sense

You need clear strategy and someone senior hands-on, but not a large team. Consultants suit startups and SMEs in Bangalore, Mumbai and Delhi who want direct access to the person making decisions.

04

When a freelancer makes sense

You already know what you need and just need execution capacity in one skill.

05

The hybrid model

YKS Productions sits between consultant and agency: I set the strategy and also produce the content myself (photo, video and reels), and run campaigns on Meta, Google and LinkedIn. Specialists are added only when a project needs them. It suits brands that want senior attention without agency overhead.

06

What affects cost

Scope (number of channels), content volume, whether strategy only or full execution, contract length and ad management complexity. Compare proposals on what is actually delivered each month, not just the fee.

07

Questions to ask before hiring

Who will actually do the work? Do I own the ad accounts and content? How will results be reported? What happens if something isn’t working? Can I see real past work? (Mine includes Vaseline (Unilever), Marriott, KL Rahul and Cindrebay School of Design.)

Quick answers

Is a consultant cheaper than an agency?

Often, but compare on scope. The right question is which model delivers what you need.

Yes, that’s one of the most common setups.
