Questions, answered straight

Does YKS Talents offer modelling jobs in Jaipur?

No. YKS Talents does not offer jobs, placements or guaranteed work. It is a portfolio platform where clients casting in Jaipur and Rajasthan find the right talent. Enquiries come through YKS Talents, so your details stay private.

Is there a joining or registration fee for Jaipur talent?

No. No joining fee, no registration fee, no portfolio fee and no paid test shoot, ever. The portfolio builder is free and the PDF is yours to keep. Anyone asking for money in our name is not us.

Who can build a portfolio on YKS Talents in Jaipur?

Women aged 18 to 34 who live in or regularly work in Jaipur or elsewhere in Rajasthan, with a polished presence suited to heritage, jewellery, couture and luxury hospitality work. Every application is read personally.

What should a Jaipur portfolio show for heritage and palace shoots?

A fully styled heritage or bridal look with jewellery, a light contemporary or resort look, a close beauty frame and current natural-light digitals with exact measurements. Clients casting palace and haveli shoots look for poise and stamina as much as for the face.

Does YKS cast its own Jaipur shoots from the platform?

Yes. I cast my own YKS Productions shoots in Jaipur from YKS Talents, including heritage and jewellery work. Being on the platform makes you visible for those castings and to clients; it is not a promise of selection.

How do brands find talent in Jaipur through YKS Talents?

Send the brief with the location, the dates and the usage. You receive a shortlist of suitable Jaipur profiles and one all-in number covering talent and shoot. Contact details are never shared in either direction.
